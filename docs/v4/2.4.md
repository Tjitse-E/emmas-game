Why this matters now:

- Every v4 play by Emma on record stopped in egg 1 or soon after it. In 2.3.0-pre.1 she could not find dressing. No record shows her reaching free play. A choice of places early in the play answers the place where she stops.
- Riding is her own ask from the v4 briefing ("ride on them"). Spec 5.2 planned it for Wave B, and 2.3 put a saddle sign in the north-east as the promise.
- Tjitse's concern throughout: a solid mix of play and learning, interesting enough that she keeps playing, with a lot of reading and maths in different forms.

## 2. Goals, non-goals and anti-goals

**Goals**

- After the welcome she sees three or four places at once and picks one herself.
- Every play still has a solid mix: more reading and more maths moments than 2.3.1, in more forms.
- She can ride a unicorn out of the meadow, read signposts to choose the road, and find a wild egg.
- A play lasts until bedtime at 30 minutes of play, and she has enough to do until then.

**Non-goals**

- No herd that persists between plays. The save keeps no herd today, and Tjitse did not ask for it.
- No saddle build (spec 5.2's Building Day). The saddle is open from the first 2.4 play.
- No chariot, no free riding in the meadow, no new wardrobe items, no doubles, no dressing the babies in 3D, no fence move. 2.3's list "for 2.4" waits.
- No new learning engine. Signposts reuse the door visit's form and the hint ladder; the star egg reuses the egg panel and its plan.

**Anti-goals (a failure even if it works)**

- A play where she only rides, dresses or slides.
- A hub that confuses a 6-year-old who cannot read instructions: too many bubbles, bubbles that move while she decides, or a place that starts because she walked past it.
- Anything that breaks her saves from 2.3: her look, owned items, ribbons, levels or plays.
- A miss that is more fun than a right answer, or a cue that gives the answer before she reads.
- She gets lost or stuck on the trail and needs a grown-up.
- It stutters, reloads or leaks on her iPad in a 36-minute play.

## 5. The design

### 5.1 The play's shape

`PARTS` becomes wardrobe, welcome, hub, bedtime, goodnight. The hub (a new `src/game/hub.ts`) takes over `afternoon()` from quests.ts: its bubbles, badges, keep-clear, place taps, quiet prompts and the `RUN` table that returns framed. The welcome no longer puts the egg down or sets the nest goal, except on the first play ever (5.2); it ends by starting the hub. `?at=hub` jumps there, and the old part names stay as aliases (section 8, flows).

### 5.2 The choice hub

**Places.** A new type `HubPlace` (my call): the four gift quests keep `QuestId`, so the save and the rotation do not change. The bubble pictures are the whole choice for a child who cannot read, so they are set here (my call).

**Slots and refill (my call).**

- The hub shows three learning bubbles and at most one play bubble: three or four at once, as the sketch says.
- A bubble stays until she does its place or its cutoff passes (5.6). Nothing moves or swaps while she decides. The only swap is a play bubble's, at a refill between places (below).
- A place is **left** in a refill when it can start in that refill: its limit is not reached, its cutoff has not passed (5.6), it is not the kind of place she has just finished (a second ride or a second wish waits for one other place), and the shore egg only once it has landed. The just-finished rule gives way when it would leave no learning bubble: when ride 2 or wish 2 is the only learning place left, it comes back at once. So while a learning place can still start, the hub always shows one.
- An empty learning slot is filled in this order, with the first place that is left and does not show yet:
  1. **A missing strand.** When no shown bubble has maths and a maths place is left, the first maths place of the list below comes next; the same for reading. The egg and the ride have both (their sums, and their newborn's naming and wish); the picnic and a wish are reading; the maths quest is maths.
  2. The egg: the nest egg, or the shore egg once it has landed.
  3. The ride.
  4. The picnic or the maths quest: the one whose strand the bubbles lack, else the one of the strand she has done fewer moments of this play (a tie goes to reading).
  5. A herd baby's wish.
- Each kind of place shows at most one bubble, so two wish bubbles never show at once (my call).
- The pick is a pure function of the hub state, tested: when a reading place and a maths place are both left, the shown set holds both. When the only maths place still to come is the kind she has just finished (ride 2 right after ride 1), the set can lack maths for that one refill, and the next refill brings it first.
- Opening set from play 2: the nest egg, the ride and the picnic.
- **The shore egg lands** once the nest egg is done and one more learning place after it, or at once after the nest egg when no other learning place is left. It lands between places, never during one: a moonbeam and a sparkle at the shore, Lumi's "Oh! Another egg!" (`lumi4-another-egg`), and its bubble pops in, at the screen edge when the shore is off screen. The camera stays with her; there is no cut-scene (my call).

**Pond and dressing open after a learning place** (Tjitse's sketch; the details are my call). Each finished learning place earns one play bubble when none shows: the Corner's while she has not been to the Corner (once a play), else the pond's. The Corner's comes with Lumi's `lumi-dressing-open`, the pond's with "Let's go to the pond!" (`g4-pond`). Each line plays only the first time its bubble shows in a play. A play bubble waits until she takes it; tokens do not pile up. Picking the pond or the Corner never earns the next play bubble.

- **The Corner never blocks the pond.** When a learning place ends and the Corner's bubble is still waiting, the pond's bubble takes its place at that refill. If she leaves the pond's bubble too, the Corner's comes back after the next learning place. So while she skips the Corner the two take turns, and she gets the pond after her second learning place. Once she has been to the Corner, the pond's bubble simply waits. The swap happens between places, so nothing moves while she decides.

### 5.3 Babies in any order

The fixed order is built into the baby index today (eggs-part.ts, level.ts, quest-kit.ts `guests`). 2.4 changes it so any place can come first.

- `g.babies` is in hatch order, up to four a play: two home eggs and two trail eggs. Door = 13 + index. `Baby.door` becomes a number; `NEWBORN_DOORS` 13 to 16.
- Each egg has its own hatch spot: the nest, the pond shore, the trail clearing.
- Each new baby's look avoids every earlier baby's look this play.
- A naming leaves out every name given this play. The play's first naming has 3 wrong gems, every later one 4.
- **The newborn's first wish (my call).** It runs in its own place, right after its naming, beside her, as 2.3's wish 1 and wish 2 do: at the nest, the shore or the trail clearing. So a newborn's wish never takes a bubble, and an egg or a trip stays one place.
- **Guests (my call).** The picnic, the bead tree and the Corner gate take her newest named babies, then her followers, then the herd babies nearest the place. No quest needs two named newborns any more, and nothing reads a gap in `g.babies`.
- **Wish kinds (my call).** A wish takes the kind (dress, bath or picnic) the play has read least (a tie goes dress, bath, picnic), never the kind of the last word read, and never picnic while the picnic shows or just after it. So the nest egg's baby still wants to dress up like her. The dress-up line stays before a dress wish. Every kind leaves out its earlier basket words this play (picnic now too). `secondWishKind` goes.
- **Egg kinds.** The nest egg is warm, the shore egg snow, a trail egg a star egg (5.5). `first` (the kind's intro) is true only for each kind's first egg of the play.
- **No sum twice (my call).** `freshCell` cannot keep this: it swaps only between cells with the same place and answer (eggs.ts:641), and a middle or hard sum has one sum per answer. So `pickCells` (eggs.ts:621) leaves out, per kind and grade, the answers this play has already asked, and renormalises the triples. Leaving out an answer leaves out every sum with it, so no sum comes twice. The hay rack's sum counts as a warm sum of its grade (7 or 8 easy, 9 middle, 10 hard). A snow or star egg also leaves out every sum whose take-away fact line this play has already said, whichever egg said it, as both say the `num-take` facts. For a second star egg 2 easy, 3 middle and 4 hard answers are left, so three different answers always fit. The egg 2 "plan egg 1 first" shortcut for `?at=egg2` goes.
- **Second eggs and the guess bounds (my call).** Leaving cells out and renormalising passes the 19.1 limits only narrowly on a second egg: in its easy sum, nearest five wins 34.1% in band A, against the test's 35% and the plan's own 29% cap. The 19.1 guess-bound tests run again on the second egg of each kind (8). If one fails, that egg's plan is fitted again without the left-out cells, as `eggPlan` fits a plan now (a few milliseconds, cached per kind, band and left-out set, run at the refill and never during a walk). If a refit still misses a bound, that grade leaves out only the sums and facts already asked, not their answers, so an easy answer can come again with its other total.
- **Streams** are named by place and count (`egg-nest`, `egg-shore`, `egg-trail-1`, `hatch-1`, `naming-1`, `wish-3`, `sign-1`), so the same seed and the same choices give the same play.
- **Two egg actors:** the home egg (nest or shore, one at a time) and the trail egg. The egg tap in input.ts checks both. The second costs 2 calls and about 800 triangles, only when in view. The soft batch's room in the batch test (16 parts for one egg, tests/actors.test.ts:775) doubles.
- Counts: `herdCount(12 + n)` at goodnight, the end screen shows 12 + n, `HERD_SIZES` 12 to 16. The clips exist (`num-herd`, `num-movein`, `num-visit` reach 20 or more).

### 5.6 Bedtime at 30 minutes

**The clock.** Bedtime starts at 30:00 of play time (Tjitse: "bedtime after 30 minutes instead of 20"). It is one constant, `BEDTIME_MS = 30 * 60_000` in hub.ts, and the cutoffs below derive from it.
