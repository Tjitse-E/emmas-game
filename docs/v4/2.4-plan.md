## Global Constraints

- `BEDTIME_MS = 30 * 60_000` in `src/game/hub.ts`; it is the only bedtime number, and every cutoff derives from it.
- Cutoffs: no ride starts from 26:00, no egg from 27:00, no other place from 29:00 (every bubble goes, `g4-soon-bed` once when nobody speaks, the sky turns golden over 60 s); bedtime from 30:00 once no place runs and the wardrobe is closed. Done early's last check is at 28:00 and its wait is 2 minutes. With `?bed=<seconds>` they sit 4, 3, 2 and 1 minutes before it with a 2-minute wait, or, when it is shorter than 5 minutes, at 87%, 90%, 93% (the last check) and 97% of it with a wait of 7%.
- Play time: frame deltas capped at 1 s, from the Play tap, without hidden time, the start mirror (45 s, `MIRROR_MS`) and the Corner's music box (60 s, `CORNER_BOX_MS`; `cornerLimitMs` goes). `FREE_WAIT_MS` = 40 minutes. On a wall clock bedtime starts at most about 31:45 after Play.
- Done early (5.6): checked only at a refill after a place ends, before the last check (28:00), never while a place runs. When no learning place can start any more, the hub shows a pond bubble only if the place that just ended was a learning place (or a pond bubble was waiting), and the Corner's if she has not been: the one time two play bubbles show. A wait starts at that refill; it counts only while no place runs and starts again at the end of each place. Bedtime starts once no place runs and no play bubble is left, when the wait reaches 2 minutes, or at 30:00. Nothing else starts bedtime before 30:00.
- The hub: 3 learning bubbles and at most 1 play bubble (2 only after done early), at most one bubble per kind, bubbles pop in 0.4 s apart with a sparkle; an edge bubble is 100 design px without tail space (90 px at 1080 × 740); every target at least 64 px.
- Left (5.2): a place is left when its limit is not reached, its cutoff has not passed, it is not the kind just finished, and (the shore egg) it has landed. The just-finished rule gives way when it would leave no learning place: ride 2 or wish 2 comes back at once when it is the only learning place left. While a learning place can start, at least one learning bubble shows. The shore egg lands after the nest egg and one more learning place, or at once after the nest egg when no other learning place is left.
- Play bubbles: a finished learning place earns one when none shows, the Corner's while she has not been to the Corner, else the pond's; a waiting one swaps for the other at the next learning place while she has not been to the Corner. `lumi-dressing-open` and `g4-pond` play the first time each shows in a play.
- Limits a play: nest egg 1, shore egg 1, herd baby's wish 2, picnic 1, maths quest 1, ride 2, Corner 1. A pond visit per pond bubble: 3 minutes (`POND_VISIT_MS`) or 6 slides (hers and her babies'), or 30:00; a slide under way finishes.
- The nudge comes after 90 s of hub time with no place running since the last place ended (touches do not reset it; it waits during a pond visit), then every 90 s; quiet prompts every 30 s without a touch. Walking near starts a place only at the end of a walk, within 2.5 m of an offered place, after its bubble shows: never on the way past it and never where the last place left her (the Corner stand is 1.9 m from the picnic rug).
- Babies: up to 4 a play in hatch order, door = 13 + index (`NEWBORN_DOORS` 13 to 16), `HERD_SIZES` 12 to 16; the first naming has 3 wrong gems, every later one 4.
