# Moon Meadow World 2.2.0: design

Made on 27 October 2026 from Emma's feedback on the demo (Tjitse: she wants to dress Princess Emma when the game starts, and she still guesses instead of reading). Five idea explorers, three judges and one synthesis produced this design; Claude settled the open points below. Earlier design: [spec.md](spec.md), [demo.md](demo.md).

- **Claude did the first picture check.** The build lane looked at every new word's picture and recorded `ok`, `decoy` or `drop` in `games/moon-meadow-world/tools/picture-check.json` with `"by": "claude"`. ## 1. Summary

- **Dress Princess Emma.** After Play, Emma gets a dressing screen before Lumi's welcome. There are five rows of pictures in the order she named them: hair colour, eyes, what she wears, hair style and hair things. She taps or slides along a row. Princess Emma changes at once in 3D on the left, shows off each choice, and the camera comes close to her face for eyes and hair things. The big pink play button means "done". Her look is saved, so on the next play one tap on play is enough.
- **Princess wardrobe.** There are 6 hair colours (including candy pink and rainbow unicorn hair) and 6 eye colours. There are 6 gowns: two party dresses, two ball gowns, a fairy dress with wings and a tutu. There are 4 long hair styles, and 6 hair things (bows, a unicorn clip, flower clips, star clips, butterflies, hearts). Nothing sits on top of her head, because that is where Lumi's crown goes.
- **Goodnight ribbons become a collection.** Lumi's goodnight gift adds a new ribbon colour to whatever hair thing Emma wears. It never replaces her choice, and the colours stay in her wardrobe.
- **Guessing gets harder.** Wish baskets hold 4 pictures, then 5, and the wrong pictures look like the word (seal, sail, shell, soap, sand). A child who only reads the first letter drops from about 65% right to about 33%. A wrong pick now shows the word of the wrong picture and lights up the letters that differ. The sounds of the wish word come only after she waits. A reading level that is never shown follows her, so the game gets harder after three right first tries and easier after two struggles.
- **New words.** There are three wish kinds with about 85 v3 words: dress-up (her first baby wants to dress up like her), pond toys and a picnic. Emma checks the pictures before they go in.
- **Maths gets harder.** Eggs show 4 or 5 number stones. The wrong stones are real mistakes (one off, the number she just heard, only the first ten), not near neighbours. The stones wait to wake until Elizabeth has said both numbers. Sums are wider and never repeat within a play.
- **Two optional side quests in free play.** A royal picnic at Lumi's gazebo: two guests, two reads, and a rose for her hair. A hungry herd at a new hay rack by the stable: a third maths moment. The main path stays about 15 minutes.
- **Fixes from her iPad.** Every slider and press (name star, sound dots, home button, play-again moon) now ends even when the iPad loses the finger's release.

## 2. The customiser

### 2.1 Flow

1. The start screen does not change. Play is still the only first tap, and `audio.unlock()` stays inside its pointerdown.
2. `begin()` preloads the wardrobe clips first. The 3.5 s sweep runs as now, then the new part `wardrobe` runs (the first entry of `PARTS`). It needs no world change.
3. The camera eases into the body portrait (900 ms). The panel slides in from the right (400 ms). Input turns on only after the slide, so no target moves under a finger. Princess Emma stands at START facing the camera with the crown off and waves. Lumi stays hidden and the home button stays.
4. Elizabeth says `g4-ready` "Let's get you ready, Princess Emma!" on every play. On the first visit only she adds `g4-look-tap` "Tap a picture to change your look."
5. Emma plays freely. Nothing is checked and nothing is refused.
6. First visit only: after 8 s without a touch, the ghost hand slides along the gown rail across three gowns (Princess Emma really changes) and back to her own gown. Any touch stops it and puts her look back to what it was.
7. After 25 s without a touch, Elizabeth says `g4-ready-play` "When you are ready, tap play." once. The panel never closes by itself.
8. Done (pointerdown) runs the new `curtsy` emote and the `chime` sfx, then `fb-lovely` "Lovely!". The look is saved and `seen.wardrobe` is set. The panel slides out, `releaseFrame()` runs, `hold(false)`, and the part goes on to `welcome`.
9. The customiser opens after every Play, with her saved look selected.

From the second play on, the welcome is shorter. Lumi says `lumi-hello-back` "Hello, Emma!", the crown appears at once with a sparkle, then `g-egg-waiting` plays. The crown close-up, the ghost tap and the walk line are skipped, which saves about 35 s.

During the part, `g.free` is false so canvas taps do not walk her. The wardrobe is its own snapshot field, not a moment, because `input.ts` blocks world taps and drives the hint ladder from `snapshot().moment`. Every herd unicorn whose graze home is within 6 m of START moves to a temporary home behind the camera: (-12, 7) goes to (-14, 12) and (-7, 9) goes to (-4, 12). They go back after Done.

### 2.3 The rail control (touch rules)

Every rail is a static element and never moves. The cell under the finger comes from `clientX` against the rail's own rect, never from `elementFromPoint`.

- **pointerdown on a rail.** First end any stale gesture. Call `setPointerCapture` on the rail. `cell = cellAt(x, rect, n, pitch)`, clamped to 0 to n-1. If the cell is a new option, select it at once with a `tick`.
- **pointermove.** Only `clientX` counts. A finger that drifts onto the next row stays on this rail while capture holds. Each new cell selects with a `tick`.
- **Applying to 3D.** Hair, eyes and ribbon apply at once. Gown and style apply 150 ms after the finger settles on a cell, so a slide over four styles does not rebuild the hair four times.
- **Ending.** The gesture ends on whichever comes first: the rail's pointerup or pointercancel, `window` pointerup or pointercancel (capture phase), `lostpointercapture`, or a new pointerdown anywhere. Selection never waits for the release. The end only speaks the word, plays the reaction (`onPicked`) and saves. The end handler runs once.
- **Ribbon colour.** If the gesture began and ended on the hair thing she already wears, and the finger never left that cell, the ribbon changes to the next colour she owns, with a sparkle. With only pink owned, it is just a sparkle. This is a single tap, never a double tap.
- **No shared locks.** The rails use the new `src/ui/press.ts` helper and never touch the `active` lock in `drag.ts`.
- **Other targets.** Done acts on pointerdown. A row icon tap acts on pointerdown: it frames that part and plays that row's reaction, with no speech.
- **Turning her.** The preview box turns her when dragged: 1 degree per 2 px, clamped to 170 degrees. After the end she eases back to face the camera in 1.5 s. A tap without a move makes her `wave`. It uses the same end rules.

Speech on release: the word clip of the option, if it has one, at most one word per 1.2 s. It never cuts Elizabeth's guidance lines. While she speaks, only the newest word waits. The printed word shows in the pill for 1.5 s. Only one-syllable words of at most five letters are printed.

Sounds: `tick` per cell, `pop` plus a 3D sparkle on the changed part at release, and `chime` on Done.

### 2.4 Preview and camera

The world camera needs no change. A pure helper `portraitFrame(her, row, screen)` in `src/game/portrait.ts` works out a `world.frame(center, width, { pitch, shiftY: 0, ms: 500 })`. Passing `shiftY: 0` stops `frame()` from turning it into a sky shot.

The camera has to look through a point at height `lookY` above her, and the rig only looks at ground points. So the helper puts the ground centre on the same line of sight, behind her:

- `d = hvis / (2 tan 19°)` is the distance to the look point.
- `ox = (1 - 2 xFrac) d tan 19° aspect` moves the look point east, so she stands at `xFrac`.
- `xFrac = (panel left / 2) / screen width`, taken from `wardrobeLayout`.
- `center = (her.x + ox, her.z - lookY / tan p)`.
- `width = (d + lookY / sin p) · 2 tan 19° · aspect`.

This gives exactly the camera at `L + d (0, sin p, cos p)` looking at `L = (her.x + ox, lookY, her.z)`.

| Frame | Rows | hvis | lookY | pitch | Result at 1080 x 740 (810) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body | hair colour, gown, style; the default | 2.08 m | 0.58 | 8° | feet at 77%, head top at 11%, 356 (389) px per metre, iris 32 (35) px, she stands at x 257 px |
| Face | eyes, hair things | 1.05 m | 1.08 | 6° | head about 390 px, iris about 63 (70) px |

- A face frame goes back to the body frame 2.5 s after the last touch on a face row. Moves ease in 500 ms.
- On a resize or rotation the game frames again, because `frame()` measures width with the aspect of the moment.
- She faces the camera position. For hair things and styles she turns 25 degrees, so a side clip or a ponytail shows, then turns back.
- `hold(true)` stops the random look-around in her idle update.
- No new light. The sun already lights her face, and adding a light would recompile every Lambert program.
- Princess Emma's own tubes go from 8 to 12 sides for every look (+384 triangles).
- `princess.portrait(true)` draws her Mid parts as Fine and her Lo parts as Mid, only while the wardrobe is open. This needs Fine capacity 48. It ships on only if `budget('wardrobe')` stays under 80 calls and 100,000 triangles at 1080 x 740 with it on. Expect about 42 calls and 85k to 95k triangles.

Reactions play on release, never during a slide:

| Row | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Hair colour | `toss` (head roll 0.25, then -0.15, then 0, over 1.0 s; the tails swing), plus sparkles in the hair colour at the head and halfway down both tails |
| Eyes | `blink` (eyes a little wider for 1 s, then one slow blink, 1.6 s). Never the happy-eye arcs that clap, jump and twirl draw over the iris |
| Gown | `twirl`. The sparkle takes the gown's main colour instead of the fixed #ffb3df |
| Style | `show` (turns +0.6 rad, holds, turns -0.6 rad, back, 1.6 s, eyes open), so the back of the hair shows |
| Hair thing | `tilt` (head roll 0.22 towards the item's side, 1.2 s, eyes open), plus a sparkle at the anchor |
| Done | `curtsy` (knees bend 0.08 m, dress flare 1.1, head bows 0.25 rad, 1.2 s) |

### 2.5 Options per row

There are five rows with at most six options each. Nothing is paged, locked or greyed, and there are no text labels. The first option in every row is today's Princess Emma.

**Hair colour** (base / light). Each colour reaches the cap (Fine), the four fringe puffs and two side locks (Mid), the tubes (`HairTubes.recolour`), the plait lobes and the bun parts. It costs no draw call and no triangle.

1. sunny #ffdf86 / #fff0b8 (default)
2. strawberry #f9b08a / #ffd6bd
3. chestnut #9a6142 / #c99a72
4. midnight #3d3346 / #6f6180 (a soft black, so the bands and rim still show at night)
5. candy pink #ff9ccc / #ffd3ea
6. rainbow: the cap is #fff0c8. The tubes fade along their length through #ff9ccc, #ffc49a, #ffe98a, #9fe8c2, #9cd3ff, #c4a6ff. The fringe puffs are #ff9ccc, #c4a6ff, #9cd3ff, #9fe8c2. Plait lobes cycle the six colours.

No gold: Lumi's crown (#f5a623) would vanish against it. A unit test checks that every hair colour differs from the crown in lightness or hue.

**Eyes** (iris colour, `c.iris`). The iris shader darkens the centre, so the pupil stays visible.

1. blue #4f8fe0 (default)
2. green #3fa66a
3. brown #9a6238
4. hazel #b7832f
5. grey #7f93a6
6. violet #9a6cf0

No red or pink eyes.

**Gown.** There are three lathe shapes. Each is built once, lazily, with 22 rows x 24 and a colour attribute of item size 3. A change swaps the cached geometry on the same material, then rewrites the colours by row role (trim, main, deep, sash, bodice, tier). No new program is compiled.

2. **high:** one `Ponytail(9, 0.13)`. Tie at (0, 0.10, -0.26) and flare at (0, 0.03, -0.34) in head space. Tube peak 0.10, tip 0.03, draw range of one tail. The crown moves 0.015 m forward with this style.
3. **plaits** (word: braid): two `Ponytail(6, 0.12)`. Tie at (±0.20, -0.02, -0.10) and flare at (±0.24, -0.12, -0.14). The tubes are hidden. There is a small ribbon bow at each tip (0.6 times today's bow).
4. **bun** (word: bun): a Fine bun of radius 0.12 at (0, 0.04, -0.26), below the crown band, with 7 Lo twists round its base in the light colour. Two ringlets, each `Ponytail(4, 0.07)` tied at (±0.22, -0.06, 0.02), are drawn in the two tube slots with radius 0.03 to 0.018 and a strong 8-wave bulge, so they read as curls.

No style uses head-space y above 0.18 inside the crown ring.

**Hair things.** Every thing sits on the two side anchors `onHead(±0.8, 0.45)` (today's bow spots) for every style. Every thing also has two Lo ribbon tails in her ribbon colour, so Lumi's "new ribbons for your hair" is always true.

**Swatches** are inline SVG built by `src/ui/swatches.ts` from the same catalogue:

- Hair: a round blob in the base colour with a shine in the light colour. Rainbow uses six bands.
- Eyes: a white almond shape with the iris colour, a darker ring, the pupil and two highlights.
- Gown: a mini silhouette of the shape in its colours. Wings are drawn white, and the tutu shows white legs.
- Style: a skin-coloured head with the style shape filled in the current hair colour. It updates live.
- Hair things: the icon drawn in the current ribbon colour.

**Row icons** are also inline SVG, so there is no text: a lock of hair in the current colour with a paint drop, an eye, a gown on a hanger, a head with a ponytail, and a bow. The v3 `braid.webp` and `hair.webp` are not used as icons, because they look alike and "braid" heads a row that includes ponytails.

### 2.6 PrincessLook type and actor API

The types live in `src/contracts.ts`. Ids and colours live in the new shared `src/wardrobe.ts`, which all lanes may import, like `layout.ts`. The 3D data lives in `src/actors/looks3d.ts` and `src/actors/hair.ts`.

```ts
export type HairColour = 'sunny' | 'strawberry' | 'chestnut' | 'midnight' | 'candy' | 'rainbow';
export type EyeColour = 'blue' | 'green' | 'brown' | 'hazel' | 'grey' | 'violet';
export type Gown = 'rose' | 'rainbow' | 'lilac' | 'moon' | 'fairy' | 'tutu';
export type HairStyle = 'ponytails' | 'high' | 'plaits' | 'bun';
export type HairThing = 'bows' | 'unicorn' | 'flowers' | 'stars' | 'butterflies' | 'hearts';
export type Ribbon = 'pink' | 'lilac' | 'sky' | 'mint' | 'gold' | 'coral';
/** Ids only, never colours, so palettes can be tuned without breaking a save. */
export interface PrincessLook { hair: HairColour; eyes: EyeColour; gown: Gown; style: HairStyle; thing: HairThing; ribbon: Ribbon }
export type LookRow = 'hair' | 'eyes' | 'gown' | 'style' | 'thing';
export type PrincessEmote = 'wave' | 'clap' | 'jump' | 'twirl' | 'toss' | 'blink' | 'show' | 'tilt' | 'curtsy';

export interface Princess {
  // existing: position, mode, walkTo, stop, rideSlide, face, crown
  readonly look: PrincessLook;
  /** Cheap on every slider step: colours at once, cached dress geometry, new chains only on a style change. No new materials, programs or per-frame allocations. */
  setLook(look: Partial<PrincessLook>): void;
  emote(kind: PrincessEmote): Promise<void>;
  /** Stand still for the portrait: no random look-around. */
  hold(on: boolean): void;
  /** Turn from the current heading (preview drag); face() eases back. */
  turnBy(rad: number): void;
  /** World points for sparkles and framing. */
  point(part: 'head' | 'eyes' | 'thing' | 'dress', out?: THREE.Vector3): THREE.Vector3;
  /** A day-only extra (the picnic rose), drawn beside her hair thing. Never saved. */
  extra(kind: 'rose' | null): void;
  /** Finer shapes while the camera is close (wardrobe only). */
  portrait(on: boolean): void;
  // ribbons(colour) is removed: goodnight calls setLook({ ribbon }).
}
```

`src/wardrobe.ts` exports:

- `LOOK_ROWS`: an ordered `{ row, options: { id, swatch, word?: { clip, print } }[] }[]`
- `DEFAULT_LOOK = { hair: 'sunny', eyes: 'blue', gown: 'rose', style: 'ponytails', thing: 'bows', ribbon: 'pink' }`
- `normaliseLook(raw)`: falls back per field for unknown or missing ids
- `RIBBON_HEX`, `GIFT_ORDER`, `nextRibbon`
- `wardrobeClipIds()`

The capacity rises come before any hair thing:

| Batch | Today | New |
|---|---|---|
| Mid | 160 | 224 |
| Lo | 480 | 640 |
| Cone | 64 | 112 (star clips use 30) |
| Fine | 8 | 48 (only if the portrait detail ships) |
| Princess `PartList` | 120 | 192 |

The busiest look is about 120 parts: plaits, fairy wings, star clips and the rose. `Batch.add` and `PartList.push` log a one-time dev console warning when they drop a part. Today they drop parts silently, and herd parts would vanish because her list is flushed first.

The busiest look adds about +3.6k triangles and 0 draw calls in the follow view.

### 2.7 The save

One module, `src/game/save.ts`, uses one key, `mmw.save`. v3 shares the origin but uses its own keys. Every read and write is in try/catch. A missing, blocked or broken entry gives the defaults and a first visit. `?fresh=1` keeps the save in memory only.

```ts
interface SaveV1 {
  v: 1;
  look: PrincessLook;                       // ids
  ribbons: Ribbon[];                        // earned, in order (pink is always owned)
  plays: number;                            // plays started
  seen: { wardrobe: boolean };
  reading: { level: 1 | 2 | 3; recent: Outcome[] };   // last reading results
  last: { words: string[]; totals: number[] };       // last play's wish targets and maths totals
}
```

- `parseSave(raw)` is pure. It normalises field by field, so a newer or older shape keeps every field it understands. A later version migrates from `v`.
- **When it writes:**
  - on Play (`plays + 1`)
  - 300 ms after each released pick
  - on Done
  - after each reading result
  - at the goodnight gift, just after the starfall, before `location.reload()`
  - at the end screen (`last`)
- Emma's home server, the test server on 8785 and a renamed host are separate origins. Safari can clear script storage after 7 days without a visit. In both cases she starts fresh with the defaults: the middle reading level and the first-visit customiser.
- Update the README line "Nothing is saved".

## 3. Reading and anti-guessing

### 3.1 Exact rules

**Reading level** (`src/game/reading.ts`, a pure fold, stored in the save; nothing on screen shows it):

- A first play ever starts at level 2. Each play's first wish is one level lower, as a warm-up.
- It moves up after 3 `first` results in a row and down after 2 results in a row that are `miss` or `helped`. The history clears after each step, and the level stays between 1 and 3.
- Every checked reading moment feeds it: the 3 wishes and the 2 picnic guests. `?rl=` forces a level.

| Level | Basket | Planner | Targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 items | target, 1 with the same first letter, 1 rhyme (same rime, other first letter) or else 1 with the same last letter, 1 any | strong words of grapheme tier 1 or 2 only (v3 phase2 and phase3-4 packs) |
| 2 | 5 items | the full order below | strong words, any tier |
| 3 | 5 items | the full order below | strong words that have a twin or a frame word in the pool. With none, it plays as level 2 and logs 2 |

The full order (`planBasket(target, pool, level, rand)` in `src/content/wishes.ts`):

1. The twin from v3 `content/pairs.json`, if it is in the pool.
2. A frame word (same first and last letter, another vowel).
3. Same first letter until 3 items share it.
4. A rhyme with another first letter.
5. Same last letter while fewer than 2 share it.
6. Same letter count while fewer than 2 match.
7. Any word.

Each basket follows these rules:

- No two words from one look-alike group. There are new groups next to feed.ts's (listed in 3.2).
- The same picture never shows twice.
- Every item comes from the wish's own pool.
- The 11 words without an ok clip (sun, fig, wok, bee, pair, pea, tea, sea, tie, fir, hay) never appear.
- The basket is shuffled.
- The planner returns `decoys: Record<word, 'twin' | 'frame' | 'onset' | 'rhyme' | 'ending' | 'length' | 'other'>` for the log.
- "First letter" means the printed letter (s for sh), because that is what a guesser sees.

A **strong word** has at least two other pool words, from other look-alike groups, with the same first letter, and at least one with the same last letter and a different first letter. Only strong words become targets. `tools/build-content.ts` stops when a kind has fewer than 6 strong targets after the picture check.

Expected effect at 5 items with strong targets (simulated over the new pools):

| Guessing strategy | Today | New |
|---|---|---|
| First letter only | 64% to 71% | about 33% |
| Last letter only | 85% to 97% | 45% to 48% |
| Word length | decides some baskets | never decides |

At level 3 a reader of the first and last letter still needs the vowel, about 50%.

**A miss: the light contrast.** It changes v3's rule that one miss gives the focused hint (spec 3.3). It ships (section 0), and `?contrast=0` restores v3's rule.

1. The wrong item bumps the baby, the baby shakes its head, and `foal-silly` plays, as now.
2. The item flies home and gets a small white tag inside its cell. It is absolutely positioned at the bottom, so nothing reflows. The tag shows its own word in Andika, 34 design px.
3. Elizabeth says that word (its v3 clip, for example "Sail."). She has acted, so this is allowed, and it is not the deciding word.
4. The units that differ glow gold in the tag and in the bubble word, from a `printUnits` diff by position. When the unit counts differ, everything from the first difference glows.
5. Then `fb-letters` "Look at the letters again." plays. No sounds of the wish word play.

The ladder changes for reading only (`HintRunner` option `missStep: 'contrast'`):

- The first miss runs the contrast. It sets level 1 and is not "hinted".
- The next idle gives the focused hint (the slow sounds), and the answer then counts as helped.
- A second miss brings the ghost, as now.
- A right answer after only the contrast logs `miss`. Tjitse then sees "guessed, then read" apart from "needed the sounds".
- The tag stays until the moment ends.

**Idle wait:** 10 s with 4 items, 14 s with 5 (`idleDelay(strand, n)`).

## 4. Maths

### 4.1 Stones

The tray holds 4 stones in band A and 5 in band B. Stones are 3 to 9, distinct, ascending, and include the answer.

Wrong stones are picked by kind in this order (each is used only if it is valid, unique and not the answer):

| Kind | Warm egg and hay rack | Snow egg |
|---|---|---|
| slip | answer ± 1 | answer ± 1 |
| heard | have (the warm cups or bundles she just heard) | need |
| first-nest | 10 - have | 10 - need |
| second-nest | need - 10 | have - 10 |
| near | answer ± 2, then ± 3 | answer ± 2, then ± 3 |

- The slip and the heard number are always in the tray when valid.
- The other wrong stones come from the rest, with a weighted pick towards a target place drawn evenly from 0 to n-1. Over 10,000 eggs each place is right 1/n ± 7 points of the time.
- No simple rule (biggest, smallest, middle, nearest five) beats 35% with 4 stones or 30% with 5.
- `EggSpec.decoys` keeps the kinds for the log.

Example: warm, need 14, have 6, answer 8 gives stones 4, 6, 7, 8 (+9).

**Sleeping stones:**

- While the intro and the goal play, the stones are dimmed (opacity 0.55, desaturated) and sit 10 design px lower.
- A touch makes a stone wiggle with a `tick`. It does not drag and does not cut Elizabeth. `drag.ts` gets `onBlocked()` for this.
- When the goal clips end, the stones brighten and hop one after another (60 ms apart) with `chime`. The ladder opens at that point, so decision time starts at wake.
- Then `afterGoal` and the first-egg ghost demo run as now. A later replay never puts them back to sleep.

**Tray layout:**

- `.stone-tray.n4`: today's cells, top fixed at 112 design px below the home button, height 672, ending at 784 of 820.
- `.stone-tray.n5`: cells scaled 0.8 (stone 96 x 83, cell 106 x 117, gap 10), height 663, from 112 to 775. A stone is 87 CSS px at 1080 x 740.
