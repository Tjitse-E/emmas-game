# Unicorn Egg Nest v3: brainstorm report

For Tjitse, 2026-10-12. Read this to choose the direction. The detail lives in the [research](research/), the three [concepts](concepts/) and the 59 brainstorm ideas: [roleplay](brainstorm/roleplay.md), [collecting](brainstorm/collecting.md), [world-levels](brainstorm/world-levels.md), [maths](brainstorm/maths.md), [reading](brainstorm/reading.md) and [wildcards](brainstorm/wildcards.md). [concepts/home.md](concepts/home.md) section 0 lists which ideas were kept, merged and dropped.

**Recommendation.** Build Moon Meadow Home. Emma turns an empty meadow into a home for her herd, and each step she learns lights a stone for the next building. Add the best parts of the other two concepts and the fixes in section 3. Build it with Svelte 5 on the DOM, not Phaser 4. Patch v2 this week so her names are no longer refused. The v3 first playable, with her v2 herd, the naming fix and the pins, reaches her iPad in about build week 11 (around the end of December 2026). At 1 to 1.5 hours of play a week, the castle end (Library, Moon Tower, Throne Room) is likely in year 2.

## 1. What drives the design

- Keep the egg, hatch and name loop she ran more than 10 times in one go. Never refuse a safe name.
- Give her unicorns more to do: role play places, pins and dressing up.
- The reading and maths must be felt, and every goal must be clear. These were the v1 failures.
- Visits of 5 or 45 minutes whenever she wants. Saves on the iPad and on odin.vendic.cloud. Unicorns only, with Queen Lumi.

The [v2 audit](research/v2-audit.md) found one cause for the refused names: every name needed a pre-made Elizabeth clip, so the gold bell accepted only 25 names. It also found a hidden cap of 12 babies, which she may have hit.

## 2. The three approaches

**The Moon Kingdom** ([concepts/kingdom.md](concepts/kingdom.md)). Emma wakes a misty kingdom land by land on a map. Each land owns one block of the three ladders and brings its own breed, eggs, activities and pins. It has the strongest collecting hook (a Breed Book), but the map adds menus, the role play she wants comes late, and the art bill is the largest.

**Moon Meadow Home** ([concepts/home.md](concepts/home.md)). Emma builds a home for her herd in one valley over months. Passed ladder steps light foundation stones for the next building, and herd size opens stable rows, the parade and titles. It is closest to what she did in v2 and has the most learning per hatch, but the link from a learned step to a stone may be too abstract at 6.

**Lumi's Lost Colours** ([concepts/story.md](concepts/story.md)). The Grumble Cloud sneezed the colours away, and Emma paints them back over seven chapters. Every egg paints a spot, so every visit changes the world. But it greys out her meadow, keeps sneezing her pins away, moves the story on helped answers and has no plan after month 6.

There are no separate reviews of the three concepts. The choice and the fixes below are the author's own reading of the research and of Emma's verdicts.

**Changes to home.md**

- Emma carries each new jewel from her Jewel Box to the plot herself, with one drag (section 3.4). Test this with her before more is built on it.
- Her pins are never taken. Hide and seek is for a cousin's lost bell, and she gets a new pin as thanks.
- Royalty and role play come early: a dressing corner with crowns, capes and her pins in week 1 or 2, Lumi at every Building Day, Lumi's tea party in weeks 3 to 6, the first ball around month 3 with picture invitations.
- Unicorns only. Mistake reactions use unicorns, objects and weather. No frogs or birds.
- One goal per screen. No recap talk before she can act. The egg comes first.
- Checked moments stay real. No glowing "next stall". A reading choice that counts has at least three plausible options. A helped answer still hatches the egg but counts as a miss for mastery.
- A new name is never second class. Until its clip exists, lines skip the name and the baby's tag glows, as naming.md option (b) says. The baby still walks in at roll call.
- Gift gems follow sounds, not letters (section 3.9). Her own herd names are their own item type, name review (section 3.8).
- The valley view is a simple painted map with building icons on fixed plots.

### 3.1 Fantasy

Queen Lumi lives in the moon and sends eggs down on moonbeams. She gives Emma a meadow at the foot of a hill: "This meadow is yours. Will you make it a home for my unicorns?" Emma warms the eggs, names the babies, gives each a stall and builds what they need. From about month 3 she starts on the old castle on the hill: the gate and the ballroom first, then room by room. The last rooms need skills from Year 2, so the coronation in the throne room she built is likely in her second year. There is no villain and nothing is ever lost or sick. The home waits happily when she is away.

Cast: Elizabeth for all instructions and all learning content, Lumi (Posh) for story lines, babies and cousins (Flicker) for giggles and tiny fixed words. Every line with a number, a name or a learning word is Elizabeth's, including mistake lines such as "Too hot! One too many." See [VOICES.md](../../VOICES.md).

### 3.2 Core loop

**The heartbeat, about 3 minutes, with no limit.** It is the v2 "one more egg" loop, and every pass touches all three ladders. Tjitse's answer ("whenever she wants, 5 or 45 minutes") overrides [engagement.md](research/engagement.md) principle 15, which recommends a 10 to 15 minute night with one extra egg at most.

1. **Egg (maths).** The nest always holds an egg. Its kind follows her maths step. She warms it with number stones.
2. **Hatch.** A shiny baby after a first try with no hint, as a surprise. A moonflower appears in the meadow.
3. **Name (spelling).** Bells, the name garden or the gold bell. Elizabeth says the name, Emma spells it.
4. **First wish (reading).** The newborn's speech bubble in print ("nap", later "a red bow"). She brings it what it wants. Extra eggs keep this step, so a run of eggs still trains reading.
5. **Moving in (number).** "Bella is baby number nineteen. Which stall?" Checked only inside her number range. Once that range is secure, only every third move-in is a task.

v2 extra eggs skipped the wish and bedtime, and we do not know if she enjoyed those. The first playable measures eggs per visit against v2. If the pull drops, extra eggs after three in a row skip the first wish.

Then a new egg appears, and she chooses: another egg, a who's-waiting bubble, or free play. Up to three picture bubbles float over places where someone waits (a cousin at the salon, a cake at the tea garden). They never expire and never nag.

**Learning budget per visit.** At most 2 or 3 new items per strand. After about 15 checked items in one strand, that strand only serves review. After about 30 checked items in total, free play is offered first. At least one checked item in five is spelling. The bubble planner counts strands during the visit: when spelling drops below one in five, the next bubble is a real-word spelling (a sign, a jar label, a memory bubble with a spoken target) and the next Building Day sign is checked. These numbers are a design guess with no research source. Check them against the idle and accuracy logs.

**Clarity.** One goal per screen, always a picture. Lumi's moonbeam shows where to touch. Elizabeth says the goal once, and a tap on Lumi repeats it. Spelling says the target first. Reading never says the deciding word before she acts. The ghost hand shows each new gesture once. The v2 hint ladder stays. One new thing is announced per visit. Openings she has already earned wait in a queue and appear one per visit.

### 3.4 What levels are

**Openings.** One building plot is visible at a time, with a picture of what it will be. When Emma passes a ladder step, a jewel goes into her Jewel Box on her crown (a letter jewel or a number jewel). The next time she is in the meadow, the plot shows with a moonbeam and Elizabeth says once: "You have a new jewel. Bring it to the plot!" She drags it there, and it becomes a glowing stone with the jewel's picture. The jewel stays in her box. If she does not drag it, a jewel bubble waits over the plot with the other bubbles, and nothing is lost. When all stones are lit, a hammer bubble appears and she starts a Building Day: walls (checked numbers), trim (free colours, checked only when patterns are her current step), sign (spelling with a spoken target), ribbon, herd circle, new breed. Steps from her placement do not light stones.

**Key steps.** Some plots have a key step, because the building's activity needs that skill (the Market needs coins, M32). A key plot waits for its key step even when its stones could be lit. It stays in the mist, and the plot order skips ahead to the next plot without a key or with its key reached, so a slow strand never freezes the home. When every remaining plot waits on a key, small keyless builds fill the gap (castle rooms such as the Wardrobe and the Great Hall), so a passed step always lights a stone somewhere.

**Herd milestones.** Counted babies. The herd grows fast: v2 showed more than 10 hatches in one visit, and she arrives with 12 or more. The forecast below assumes 10 to 20 hatches a week, a guess to check against the logs. Milestones: the Bath Pond at 5, a stable row every 10 (she builds and names it, and names the new sky unicorn), the Post Box at 15, Letter Lane at 20, the Parade Field at 40, Royal Nest Keeper and the herd banner at 75, the Hundred Party at 100, the Show Ring at 150, then medals at 200, 300 and 500. The Parade Field waits for play month 2 and the Show Ring for play month 4, so the herd places spread out. From Row 6 on, a new row is a short build (planks to fill ten, then a name), not a full Building Day. Learning inside a herd-opened place follows her current steps. Content above her step is shown and said, never checked.

### 3.5 Role play rules

1. **Her own herd is free play.** Styling, feeding, bathing and dressing her own babies is never checked.
2. **A visitor, wish, card or envelope brings the checked work.** It arrives with a chime and a seal and stays pinned on screen while she works.
3. **Three answer tools only.** Gems in slots for spelling, stones for numbers, print with sound dots for reading. New places bring new pictures, never new controls.
4. **A mistake is a funny event, once.** A wrong seat: the baby hops on, looks at the card, "Not me!", and hops off. Then the v2 hint ladder. Nothing a mistake produces is a reward.
5. **She brings her babies.** At every door she can pick 1 to 3 babies to come along. They show up in that place's photo.
6. **A question is never the task.** The task is always an act: drag pins until the sash is full, seat the guest, fix the slate. Elizabeth says the goal, not a question to answer.
7. **Hatch-day parties do not pile up.** At most one waits at a time, picked from her favourites. A missed one does not wait for later.

The activities, their mistakes and their collections are written out in [concepts/home.md](concepts/home.md) section 4.

**Names in spoken lines.** Clips join only at sentence boundaries (home.md 10.1). So a baby's name is always its own sentence: "Pip!" then "Into the boat you go." Books, invitations, notes and plaques are written that way, and printed text can show any name. Each name needs only its two clips ("Pip!" and "Pip?"), and the sentences around it come from finite lists. A baby whose name has no clip yet is not picked for a book or an invitation until the clip exists.

### 3.6 Collecting and milestones

- **The herd.** Every baby is an individual with a name, coat, breed, traits, growth stage, stall, pins, outfit, best friend and later a job. The count is big and said often, tens first from M27. Her favourites stand in the meadow (up to 12), the rest look out of their stall doors. A second Tim is "Tim the Second" ("Tim!", then "The Second!").
- **Breed Book and egg kinds.** One breed per egg kind and per building egg, with silhouettes. Breeds differ in coat pattern, markings, mane style and horn colour on one rig. Wings come later on the same rig.
- **Pins.** A pin is always a baby's pin. Sets with silhouettes. Each baby wears a sash of ten (two rows of five). A full set changes something in the world (a full flower set puts a flower arch on the salon). Moment pins for firsts. Pins are never taken away.
- **Jewel Box.** Emma's own collection: letter jewels and number jewels for passed steps, kept in a box on her crown. A tap on a letter jewel plays its pure sound.
- **Home Book.** Picture tabs: herd in pages of ten, pins, breeds, album, recipes, books, home, sky.
- **Titles on her crown.** Nest Keeper, Stable Keeper (10 babies), Meadow Keeper (5 buildings), Royal Nest Keeper (75), Keeper of the Castle, Princess of the Moon Meadow.
- **Show Papa.** The whole herd parades, Elizabeth says every name that has a clip, and the count comes at the end.

**Name review.** Reading her own babies' names (roll call, envelopes, place cards, plaques) is its own item type. Each name has a Leitner box. Results are logged under reading, never count toward a step pass and count toward the visit total, not toward reading's 15.

### 3.9 The naming fix

From [research/naming.md](research/naming.md):

- **Three ways to pick.** Name bells at her level (fully checked). The name garden by picture or first sound (Lily, Rose, Luna), spelled with gift gems. The gold bell for any name, 2 to 6 slots, her full gem set.
- **Gift gems follow sounds.** A slot is hers only if its grapheme makes its usual sound and that sound is at or below her step. Bella's last a and Daisy's s (it says /z/) are gifts. From S21 on, any slot whose sound has more than one spelling at her level is a gift (the y in Lily, the ee in Reese). No Dutch voicing position is checked before S06. Fewer than two own slots counts as free naming.
- **The gold bell accepts every safe name.** Checks in order: no vowel ("Every name needs a pink gem!"), a blocked word (a giggle, "That's a silly name!"), a list name (Elizabeth says it), a near match (a second bell offers the list name, she picks either), otherwise a brand new name.
- **A brand new name** gets its tag at once. She hears her own sounds when she slides the star, as in v2. Until the clip exists, lines skip the name and the tag glows. On a later visit Lumi says "The moon told me a new name!", Elizabeth says the name, and the baby jumps and gets a heart.
- **Gone from v2:** "I don't know that name", the same-first-and-last-letter rule, wish words as forbidden names, the 3-slot limit.
- **Duplicates** are royal: "Tim the Second". **Every build is logged**, accepted or not, for the parent corner.

## 4. Tech recommendation

### 4.1 Engine: not Phaser 4

Use **Svelte 5 + Vite + TypeScript, rendering to the DOM**, with layered raster art animated by CSS transforms. Reasons, from [research/engine.md](research/engine.md):

- The game is touch UI, crisp words and collection screens. The DOM does this natively. A canvas engine rebuilds buttons, lists and text.
- Agents test with `playwright-cli snapshot`, which sees every DOM element. A canvas needs hand-written hooks and pixel coordinates.
- Andika text is sharp in the DOM. In Phaser it needs device-pixel tricks, and Phaser 4.2.1 has an open text bug on iPadOS 16.4 and older (#7392). Phaser 4 is six months old.
- There is no physics, no tilemap and no fast action.

Escape hatch: if 24 rigged babies stutter on Emma's iPad, the meadow alone renders with PixiJS v8 inside Svelte. A later arcade mini-game can be a separate Phaser page that shares the save.

### 4.3 Server and saves

- **odin.vendic.cloud over HTTPS** serves the static game from build week 1 (decision 6), so `persist()` and the offline cache work from the first playable. A small save service follows: one JSON save per family, with the last versions kept for rollback and a nightly copy. Access and privacy are decision 7. Tjitse deploys it. Agents write the code and a deploy note and do not SSH to odin.
- **Merge rules.** Babies get UUIDs, not counters, so two offline devices never both create baby 24. Every checked result is an event in an append-only log with its own id. A merge takes the union of both logs and rebuilds Leitner boxes and step state from it, so the mastery rule (12 of the last 15, first tries in two sessions) sees both devices. The herd, pins and the name log also merge as a union and are never deleted by a merge. Stall numbers follow hatch time after a merge, a moved baby trots to its new stall quietly, and the stall task is not asked again.
- **Name clips.** Bank clips and approved queue clips are static files on odin next to the game. The game fetches a name manifest at runtime, the service worker caches name audio on its own route, and name clips use string ids outside the typed clip list. The name queue travels inside the save.
- **Before the save endpoint exists**, the game saves to IndexedDB with `persist()`, and export and import go through the share sheet. Sync follows as soon as the endpoint is live.

### 4.4 Art pipeline

An image model makes the art, agents do the rest, and Tjitse and Emma pick the style once. The model, its cost and its licence are decision 10.

1. Generate 10 to 20 candidates of one baby and one background in a soft painted picture-book style. Lock the winner as the style reference.
2. Generate one baby in fixed poses from that reference, cut it into layers (body, head, eyes, mane, tail, legs, horn), and colour the coat and mane with masks. Put the wing layer and named anchor points for every growth stage into the rig in week one, because wings and accessories come later.
3. Animate with code tweens on the rig: breathe, blink, tail swish, hop, sleep. No Rive or Spine.
4. Items, pins and word pictures in the same style, cut out, one atlas per area. A picture check with Emma for each batch.
5. One background per place. The valley view is a simple map with building icons, not one painted scene of 25 buildings.
6. A contact sheet page and a script check every batch. Week one: the stress test with 24 rigged babies on Emma's iPad.

**Volume.** Word pictures: about 250 for R01 to R20 (about 350 words, of which tricky and function words need none), and about 1,200 to 1,500 in the first year, from about 2,000 learning words and the 30 to 40 bank words per Phase 5 step. Breeds: about 35 (one per egg kind and per building) on four growth stages, about 140 marking and colour sets on the one rig. Plus about 25 backgrounds and the item sets.
