# Game: notes

The level script (`src/game/level.ts`), the customiser part (`wardrobe-part.ts`, its `dressUp` shared with the Dressing Corner), the choice hub (`hub.ts` the pure rules, `hub-part.ts` the part, 2.4.0), the gift quests (`quests.ts`, the Dressing Corner in `corner.ts`, the shared walks, the rotation and the gifts in `quest-kit.ts`), what the moments share (`moments.ts`), touch input (`input.ts`), the door moments (`doors.ts`), shared state and helpers (`game.ts`), the save (`save.ts`), the reading level (`reading.ts`), the maths band (`band.ts`), the random streams (`streams.ts`), the address options (`options.ts`), the customiser's camera (`portrait.ts`), the results log (`results.ts`), `src/main.ts`, `src/test-hook.ts` and `index.html`.

## The level, part by part

2.4.0: the parts are wardrobe, welcome, hub, bedtime and goodnight (`PARTS`, docs/v4/2.4.md section 5.1). The hub (`hub-part.ts`) took over the fixed script; the eggs, the wishes, the pond, the gift quests, the Corner and the ride are its places.

| Part | What the script does |
|---|---|
| Start | The world is paused (`world.setPaused(true)`) behind the start screen; every shader program is compiled at boot (`renderer.compile`), the toy sprite's too: a herd unicorn holds a toy meanwhile. Her saved look is applied at boot. The Play tap unpauses before the fade and counts a play in the save. The customiser's clips are preloaded first. The sweep takes 3.5 s. |
| Wardrobe | The start mirror (`wardrobe-part.ts`): 2.2.0's customiser on the 2.3 contract, with a 45 s music box (`MIRROR_MS`, decision 1; `?box=`) and the crown only once Lumi has given it. `g.clock.mirror` is true while it is open, so the play clock stands still. `g.free` is false, Lumi hidden, `hold(true)` and `portrait(true)` (not with `?portrait=0`). The two herd unicorns grazing near START graze behind the camera until Done. Done: the look is saved with `seen.wardrobe`, a `kind=look` diag line goes out, she curtsies while the layer says "Lovely!". |
| Welcome | `opening({ plays, seen })` (level.ts) decides. First play ever, or a save without the crown: the long welcome as in 2.3, ending with the nest egg, "An egg is waiting.", the ghost tap and "Tap the grass to walk."; the hub then starts with the nest egg asked. From the second play: "Hello, Emma!", the crown at once with a sparkle, and the hub at once (no egg). |
| Hub | See "The hub" below. |
| Bedtime | The babies lane's (`bedtime.ts`). The hub ends when `bedtimeDue`: 30:00 of play time with no place running and the wardrobe closed, or done early. |
| Goodnight | The babies lane's (`bedtime.ts`). |

## The customiser (`wardrobe-part.ts`, `portrait.ts`)

- `layer.wardrobe(o)` gets the saved look, the owned ribbons (`['pink', ...save.ribbons]`), `firstTime: !seen.wardrobe` and the clips `ready`, `lookTap`, `readyPlay`, `lovely` from `CLIPS`.
- `onLook(look, row)`: `princess.setLook(look)` at once (the layer delays gown and style by 150 ms itself).
- `onPicked(look, row)`: the row's reaction (`toss`, `blink`, `twirl`, `show`, `tilt`), a sparkle at `princess.point()` of the changed part in its new colour, and a save 300 ms later (a quick second pick saves once).
- `onFocus(row)`, and also `onLook` and `onPicked` for their row: eyes and hair things bring the face frame, the other rows the body frame (500 ms moves). After eyes, hair things and styles, the camera and her 25 degree side turn (hair things and styles, so a side clip or a ponytail shows) go back 2.5 s after the last touch there; `onFocus(null)` starts that wait again. Otherwise she faces the camera.
- `onTurn(dxPx, ended)`: `dxPx` is the finger's horizontal move since the last call (as the ui sends it). 1 degree per 2 px, at most 170 degrees from facing the camera, through `princess.turnBy(delta)` with a positive angle for a finger moving right; on the end she faces the camera again (`face()` eases back).
- `onPreviewTap()`: `wave`.
- `portraitFrame(her, row, screen)` is pure and unit tested. `screen.panelLeft` comes from `panelLeftOf(w, h)`, the panel's left edge from the ui's `wardrobeLayout`. A window resize frames again at once.

## The hub (`hub.ts`, `hub-part.ts`; docs/v4/2.4.md sections 5.2 and 5.6)

`hub.ts` holds the pure rules, `hub-part.ts` runs them in the world.

- **The play clock** (`tickPlay`, `installPlayClock`): `performance.now()` deltas per rendered frame, each capped at 1 s, so hidden time never counts. It stands still while the start mirror is open (`g.clock.mirror`) and while the Corner's music box plays (`q.dressing`, which also counts as `clock.dressMs.corner`), and it counts from the Play tap. While the hub runs with no place running it also adds to `idleMs` (the play line's `fr`), `nudgeIdleMs` and, after done early, `earlyIdleMs`. A pond visit is a running place.
- **Cutoffs** (`cutoffs(bedMs)`): rides from 26:00, eggs from 27:00, every place from 29:00, done early's last check at 28:00 and its wait 2 minutes. `?bed=` under 5 minutes scales them to 87%, 90%, 97% and 93% of it with a wait of 7%. `BEDTIME_MS` is the only bedtime number.
- **The refill** (`refill`, pure): the shown learning bubbles that are still left stay; each empty slot of three fills in 5.2's order (a missing strand first, maths before reading; the egg; the ride; the picnic or the maths quest by the strand she has done fewer moments of; a herd baby's wish), at most one bubble per kind (`KIND`), then the play bubbles. A place is left (`isLeft`) when it is under its limit (`PLACE_LIMIT`), before its cutoff, landed (the shore egg), this play's (the maths quest; a wish only with a baby that can wait) and not the kind just finished, unless that would leave no learning place.
- **A place ends** (`placeEnded`): done, the nudge's and done early's counts back to 0, the shore egg lands (after the nest egg and one more learning place, or at once after the nest egg when nothing else is left), the play bubble (the Corner's while she has not been, else the pond's; a waiting one swaps for the other at the next learning place while she has not been to the Corner; a visit uses its own), and done early's check before 28:00: when no learning place can run any more, the pond's bubble (only after a learning place, or when one still waits) and the Corner's if she has not been. `bedtimeDue` after done early: no play bubble left, or the wait passed with no place running.
- **The bubbles** (`BUBBLE`): the moon egg on its nest over the nest egg (`base`), the snow egg over the shore egg, the wish basket over the waiting herd baby, the picnic, the pink bead and the hay bundle as in 2.3, the saddle over the paddock (`RIDE.stand` at y 2.4), the trunk picture over the trunk, the slide 0.9 m over its top. Badges on the gift quests. They pop in 0.4 s apart (`POP_GAP_MS`) with a sparkle on their place, hide while a place runs (all but the learning ones during a pond visit), and keep clear of her (`herBox`) and of each other. A bubble off screen waits at the edge, small (`geometry.ts edgeSize`).
- **What she hears**: the first time the Corner's bubble shows in a play Lumi says `lumi-dressing-open`, the pond's `g4-pond`. On the first hub ever (`!seen.hub`) `mw-bubbles-explain` while the ghost hand glides over each bubble (`layer.ghost.glide`, at the bubbles' screen boxes); `seen.hub` is written when it played to its end or at her first bubble tap. `g4-pick` at the opening and after her first two places, then no more. The shore egg lands with a moonbeam, a sparkle and Lumi's `lumi4-another-egg` while its bubble pops in; the camera stays with her.
- **Asking a place** (`askPlace`): a bubble tap, a tap on its place (input.ts), a tap on the waiting wish baby, or a walk that ends (`walkEnded`: walk or run to idle) within 2.5 m of an offered place whose bubble showed before the walk ended (`walkEndStart`; never a wish). During a pond visit a learning bubble ends the visit at once. A bubble or place tap that cannot start walks her there.
- **Running a place** (`RUN`, `runPlace`): the nest and shore eggs (`eggPlace`; a bubble tap walks her to the egg, the first play's forced egg keeps its goal so she walks herself), a herd baby's wish (`wishPlace`), the picnic, the bead tree and the hay rack (each with its gift in its frame, `runQuest`), the Corner, the ride (`ridePlace(g, n)`) and the pond (`pondVisit`). Then `placeEnded`, the gift quest into `q.done`, `afterPlace(g, p, { n, fromMs, checkedMs })`, the basket, and the refill.
- **Prompts and the nudge**: every 30 s without a touch, in turn, `mw-bubbles-explain`, `g4-pick`, `g4-follow`. After 90 s of hub time with no place running (touches never reset it), the nearest learning bubble along the route (a tie to the one shown longest, `firstLearning`) grows and bobs (`layer.offerBob(id, true)`), the beacon marks its place and its "this way" line plays; again every 90 s. A Lumi tap in the hub says `g4-pick` and bobs every bubble.
- **29:00**: every bubble goes, the sky turns golden over 60 s (`setTime(g, 0.3, 60_000)`), and `g4-soon-bed` once when nobody speaks and no place runs. The hub ends at `bedtimeDue` (its safety `FREE_WAIT_MS`, 40 minutes; `skip()` ends a wait without ending the hub) and writes `hub.bedtimeAt` when bedtime has not.
- **The pond visit** (`pondVisit`, `visitOver`): she walks to the ladder (a learning bubble tap on the way ends it without a visit); the visit counts as done when she arrives. The first visit of a play gets the ghost drag of a follower to the slide's top and `g4-slide-drag`. The ducks paddle to the far shore; slides (hers and every unicorn's) count at their splash. It ends at 3 minutes of play time, 6 slides or bedtime, a slide under way first; `skip()` ends it too. Then the ducks paddle back under the chute and the first learning bubble is nudged. **The resting slide** (`slideTap`, input.ts): between visits a tap on the slide makes the ducks bob, and the first time a play Lumi says `lumi4-ducks`; a unicorn dropped on its top lands beside the ladder (`BESIDE_LADDER`).
- **Wishes and followers**: when a wish bubble enters the set, `offerWish` picks the waiting herd baby (`g.waiting`); when it leaves, `withdrawWish`. The waiting baby never follows (`Followers.refuse`, set in `createGame`), is never carried (`canCarry`), and a tap on it asks its wish. The hay rack's helpers walk to the rack when its bubble shows and graze at home when it goes (`hungryHerd`, `helpersHome`).
- **The maths quest's rotation** (`mathsQuestFor`): as in 2.3; `save.afternoon` is written after each place (`afterPlace`), and the play reads its start copy (`g.start`).
- **Gifts** (`giveGift`, `wearGift`): as in 2.3.
- **Starts** (`resolveStart`, `hubAt`, `spotsOf`, `snapTo`): `?at=hub` opens the hub, `?place=` asks a place as it opens (forced: the Corner and the pond count as earned), `?step=naming|wish` jumps into an egg place's naming or wish (`askStep`, with 2.3.3's `naming` and `wish` of eggs-part.ts on a snapped baby), and a ride step goes to `snapTrip`. The old part names are aliases: `egg1` the nest egg, `egg2` the shore egg with the nest egg done, `naming1`/`wish1` and `naming2`/`wish2` their steps, `pond` the pond, `free` the hub with both home eggs done. The done places' babies are snapped in hatch order, `?babies=n` overrides them.

### The Dressing Corner (`corner.ts`, 17.4)

1. Her babies stop following and walk to `CORNER.babies` (put there after 4 s, the last step towards the camera); her other followers wait at `CORNER.aside`; the herd near the step walks behind the camera (`herdAsideAt(g, CORNER.herd, true)`). She walks to `CORNER.stand`.
2. `frameAt` the step: centre (-14, 3.0), 5.5 m wide, the first baby's head top at 45%, her crown kept.
3. Lumi: `lumi4-corner` "Your babies want to dress up first!"
4. The gate: `gateWish` asks `planWish(g, 'phrase', 'gate1' | 'gate2', { wish: false })`, then `runWish(..., { frame: false })`. With fewer than 6 strong phrases among the pairs `?phrases=` allows (every ok pair minus the iPad's crosses by default, so only after crosses that leave a colour without its baskets, or with `?phrases=emma` before her check) the reading lane's planner gives no phrase: it gives the dress-up wish itself or throws because it has no pair to pick. Then the gate asks for `planWish(g, 'dress', part, { wish: false })` itself, for both reads, and "Your baby wants to dress up like you!" plays before the first. So the Corner always opens. `q.gate` keeps the kind (word when a read was a word wish) and the first tries for the `kind=play` line.
5. The gift from the hair set (`giveGift(g, 'corner')`): she wears it into the wardrobe.
6. Her babies step to `CORNER.watch` (outside her close-up and at least 1.2 m from the followers' spots), Lumi's portrait steps away, the trunk lid opens (`world.corner.lid(true)`), and `dressUp` (wardrobe-part.ts) opens the wardrobe at her spot in the portrait frame with `board` (`boardSpec`: what she owns, today's gifts, the ribbons, each set's quest picture) and `limitMs` (`?box=`, else 60 s: `CORNER_BOX_MS`, off the play clock). `onBox` sets `q.dressing`. The Corner's panel says no `g4-ready` and no idle line ("When you are ready, tap play."): those are the start mirror's (`dressClips`), and a pause at the Corner is her dressing time.
7. The kind ending is `dressUp`'s: her look saved as it is, a `kind=look` line with `at=corner`, the curtsy while the layer chimes and says "Lovely!". The lid closes, and the quest resolves with its release (the frame, the herd home, the babies follow, Lumi back).
- **The trunk after her visit** (`trunkTap`): a tap on the trunk, the mirror or the step opens the lid on `layer.board` for 3.5 s, then closes it. It never refuses and never starts the Corner again.
- **The saddle sign** (input.ts): a tap makes the picture shimmer with a sparkle, and Lumi says `lumi4-saddle` once a play (2.4.0; never over a line that plays).

### The picnic and the hay rack (2.2.0)

- The food basket shows from the hub's first refill (2.4.0: without the basket line), hides while a place runs. A food dropped on a unicorn plays the synthesised munch (2.3.0). When the hay rack's bubble shows, the three grazing herd unicorns nearest the rack walk to its eat spots; the one picked by `randFor('rack-herd')` hops and says "I'm hungry!" as an aside when it arrives.
- The picnic: her babies stop following, her other followers wait west of the rug (`PICNIC.aside`, clear of the cushions and the close-up), the herd grazing by the gazebo walks behind the camera (`herdAside(g, true, { walk: true })`). She walks to `PICNIC.stand` (at once after 12 s), the babies to the seats (placed there after 4 s, facing the camera). The frame centres the rug 0.6 m east, 5.5 m wide, head tops at 45%. Nothing is said first (no `lumi4-picnic`, which pointed at the foods), then a wish per guest from the stream `picnic` with intro and goal `g4-give` (no warm-up; results `picnic1`, `picnic2`, strand `r`). The reward: `lumi4-picnic-done`, stars on her head, `princess.extra('rose')`, a twirl and hearts. The rose stays until the end of the play (`snapTo` takes it off). Then the gift (a crown).
- The hay rack: she walks to her spot 2.2 m west of the rack (at `HAY_RACK.stand` she stood between the camera and the rack, behind the hay panel) and turns half to the camera; the sum from the stream `rack` in the current band. Frame: 0.3 m west of the rack centre (clear of the stone tray), 6 m wide, the rack's top (y 1.5) at 28% of the height. `react('munch')` (too many; `'hot'` too) makes a helper munch, `'look'` (too few; `'cold'` too) makes the helpers look, `'right'` (or `'warm'`) fills the rack (`stable.hay(1, 1200)`) and every helper and both babies munch with hearts. 3 s later the gift (shoes), then the helpers graze at home again. Result `rack`, strand `m`; it moves the maths band.

## Reading level, maths band, streams and the save

- `reading.ts`: a first play starts at level 2; three `first` in a row move up, two `miss` or `helped` in a row move down; the history clears after each step. The play's first wish is a level lower. `?rl=` forces a level for every reading moment and the fold is then not stored.
- Every reading result (three wishes, two picnic guests) folds into `save.reading` and is written at once.
- `band.ts`: the first maths moment of a play is band A; `first` moves to B, `helped` to A, a miss and the doors leave it. `?maths=` fixes it. The total of a sum is `need` for a warm egg and the hay rack and `have` for a snow egg; every planned sum goes into `g.maths.used`.
- `streams.ts`: `g.randFor(label)` is `stream(seed, label)`, cached for the play (asking again continues it); a jump resets every stream. Labels: `egg1`, `egg2`, `rack`, `rack-herd`, `hatch1`, `hatch2`, `naming1`, `naming2`, `wish1`, `pond`, `wish2`, `picnic`, `rollcall`, `visit`. A jump's skipped babies use `hatch1`/`hatch2` and `naming1`/`naming2`, so they match a full play.
- `save.ts`: `createSave(() => localStorage, fresh)`; `parseSave` is pure and tolerant (junk, an empty store, a newer `v`, unknown ids, storage that throws); every access is in try/catch. Writes: on Play (`plays + 1`), 300 ms after a released pick, on Done, after each reading result, at each gift (what she owns and her look), after each hub place (`afterPlace`: `afternoon`, and the babies lane's `last`), at the first hub's explanation (`seen.hub`), at the goodnight gift, at the end screen (`last`). Pink is always owned and never listed in `ribbons`.

## Results and the server log

- `Results.add` logs `[mmw result]` and sends `diag.txt?kind=result&…` (at most 400 characters, `diagQuery`). Wishes: `part`, `o`, `ms`, `mi`, `lv` (level), `n` (basket size), `w`, `pt` (print touched), `q` (quick), `tried` as `word:decoy`. Maths: `band`, `k`, `need`, `have`, `a`, `st` (stones), `pl` (the answer's place, from 0), `q`, `tried` as `stone:decoy`, `dc` (every decoy). `pt` and `q` are read from any `hooks.log` event whose data has `printTouched` or `quick` booleans.
- Done in the customiser (or the end of its music box) sends `kind=look` with `at` (mirror or corner), `end` (done or time), `tabs` (the tabs she picked in), `ms` and the look's short fields.
- **`kind=play`** (2.3.0, `results.ts playFields`, sent at goodnight before the end screen): `v` the build's version, `pl` plays, `min` minutes since Play, `ck` checked s, `dr` dressing s (`mi` + `co`), `mi` mirror s, `co` the Corner's dressing s (its music box, `clock.dressMs.corner`), `cv` the whole Corner visit s (the walk, the gate, the gift and the wardrobe; its gate is checked time too), `fr` free s, `gk` the gate's kind (phrase, or word when fewer than 6 phrases are strong), `gt` gate first tries, `q` quests finished, `rl` reading level, `ph` phrase level, `mb` maths band, `pt` pattern level, `gf` gifts as field:id (last, so a long list is cut first). Under 400 characters (tested).
- Those requests 404 on every server (that is the point: the request line is the record). `flows/lib.sh` `finish()` ignores their 404 console lines; any other `diag.txt` line (a failed picture or clip) still fails a flow.

## Rules in the code

- A tap never cuts speech: a unicorn's name or "Yum!" waits its turn (`sayAside`), one at a time; further taps only show the tag and the hop.
- `setTime(g, t, ms)` sets the time of day in the world and the colour wash in the 2D layer (`layer.sky`) together.
- Results: `detail.tried` collects every wrong choice (`hooks.log('tried', { what })` from the ui moments, the door number from the door moments). A right door after the row hint (level 2) is `helped`.
- A wrong door: the unicorn inside in a nightcap ("Not me!"); a unicorn of that door still in the yard trots to its door and shakes its head ("Not me!"); an empty stall swings open and her baby giggles ("Silly!"). During a door moment a tap on a waiting unicorn makes it hop and look at its own door.
- `world.onSlow(level => actors.setDetail(level))`; the `?perf=1` box shows the pixel ratio and the quality level.
- No WebGL 2, or `createWorld` throws: `window.__mmwFallback()` from `index.html` shows a night picture with the home button. The same script shows it after 10 s when the game script never started (`main.ts` sets `window.__mmwStarted` first thing).
- Baby close-ups (naming and wishes) pass her crown to `frameAt` as `keep`, so her crown stays on screen.
- A picture shown by a baby (`showToy` in `moments.ts`) is taken away after its time only if no other picture replaced it meanwhile. A greyscale picture shows in its tint (`WishItem.tint`).
- World touches (`input.ts`) run on `press()`: a release only the window hears still taps; a cancel, a lost capture or the next touch ends the touch without a tap, and a carried unicorn lands where the finger last was. One lost release never blocks later world taps.
- During a place a Lumi tap replays an open moment's goal; between the place's moments she stays quiet (the basket is hidden).
- `Run.until()` takes its cleanup off the run when it settles (`defer`'s undo), so a 36-minute play keeps `run.deferred` level.
