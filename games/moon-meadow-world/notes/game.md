# Game: notes

The level script (`src/game/level.ts`), the customiser part (`wardrobe-part.ts`, its `dressUp` shared with the Dressing Corner), the side quests (`quests.ts`, the Dressing Corner in `corner.ts`, the shared walks and the afternoon's pure rules in `quest-kit.ts`), what the moments share (`moments.ts`), touch input (`input.ts`), the door moments (`doors.ts`), shared state and helpers (`game.ts`), the save (`save.ts`), the reading level (`reading.ts`), the maths band (`band.ts`), the random streams (`streams.ts`), the address options (`options.ts`), the customiser's camera (`portrait.ts`), the results log (`results.ts`), `src/main.ts`, `src/test-hook.ts` and `index.html`.

## The level, part by part

| Part | What the script does |
|---|---|
| Start | The world is paused (`world.setPaused(true)`) behind the start screen; every shader program is compiled at boot (`renderer.compile`), the toy sprite's too: a herd unicorn holds a toy meanwhile. Her saved look is applied at boot. The Play tap unpauses before the fade and counts a play in the save. The customiser's clips are preloaded first. The sweep takes 3.5 s. |
| Wardrobe | The start mirror (`wardrobe-part.ts`): 2.2.0's customiser on the 2.3 contract, with a 30 s music box (`MIRROR_MS`, 45 s before 2.3.0's last pass; `?box=`) and the crown only once Lumi has given it. `g.free` is false, Lumi hidden, `hold(true)` and `portrait(true)` (not with `?portrait=0`). The two herd unicorns grazing near START (homes (-12, 7) and (-7, 9)) graze behind the camera at (-14, 12) and (-4, 12) until Done. The camera eases into the body portrait (900 ms) while the layer's panel slides in. Done: the look is saved with `seen.wardrobe`, a `kind=look` diag line goes out, she curtsies while the layer says "Lovely!", then `releaseFrame()`, `hold(false)`, `portrait(false)` and the herd walks home. |
| Welcome | First play: as before. From the second play (`save.plays >= 2`): "Hello, Emma!", the crown at once with a sparkle, then "An egg is waiting." |
| Eggs | Each egg from its own stream (`egg1`, `egg2`) in the current maths band, leaving out this play's sums and, while it can, the last play's totals. The nest glow goes off as soon as she reaches the nest. She stands beside the egg at its depth and turns a three-quarter face to the camera (also in the hatch and the naming). |
| Wishes | Wish 1 (nest) is the dress-up wish: "Your baby wants to dress up like you!" first, the picture worn on the baby's head for 20 s, "Pretty!". The pond wish is the bath wish. Wish 2 (wild egg) is the picnic wish, with no line first ("I'm hungry!" before a food only would give the answer away); "Yum!" after a food, "Yay!" after a dish or a mug, which stays at the baby's feet for 8 s (the bath toy stays for the swim). The play's first wish is the warm-up, one reading level lower. |
| Pond | The tap line after 12 s without a slide, once more 20 s later, the egg comes at 45 s. After the first slide, 8 s more play, then the first baby's bath wish on the nearer of two shore spots (water behind them), result part `pond`. |
| Wild egg | The camera frames the egg's landing spot, 9 m wide. |
| Free play | The afternoon (`quests.ts`): see below. |
| Bedtime | The bubbles hide. The whole stable yard counts as the stable (`inStableYard`); a door or roof tap on the way walks her to her bedtime spot. Roll call (7 to 12, two doors), the herd goes in while both newborns move in, then the visit: a newborn visits a teen door (11 to 14). The next door is asked while the last unicorn walks in; a walk-in longer than 4 s pops the unicorn into its stall. |
| Goodnight | The yawn plays out, then "Fourteen unicorns!" while the camera tilts up to the stars. The ribbons: the gift is `nextRibbon(look, owned)` on whatever hair thing she wears (`setLook({ ribbon })`), saved at once with the new colour in `ribbons`. At the end screen the save gets `last` (this play's wish and picnic targets and maths totals). The end screen gets all 14 members, and the world pauses once it covers it. |

## The customiser (`wardrobe-part.ts`, `portrait.ts`)

- `layer.wardrobe(o)` gets the saved look, the owned ribbons (`['pink', ...save.ribbons]`), `firstTime: !seen.wardrobe` and the clips `ready`, `lookTap`, `readyPlay`, `lovely` from `CLIPS`.
- `onLook(look, row)`: `princess.setLook(look)` at once (the layer delays gown and style by 150 ms itself).
- `onPicked(look, row)`: the row's reaction (`toss`, `blink`, `twirl`, `show`, `tilt`), a sparkle at `princess.point()` of the changed part in its new colour, and a save 300 ms later (a quick second pick saves once).
- `onFocus(row)`, and also `onLook` and `onPicked` for their row: eyes and hair things bring the face frame, the other rows the body frame (500 ms moves). After eyes, hair things and styles, the camera and her 25 degree side turn (hair things and styles, so a side clip or a ponytail shows) go back 2.5 s after the last touch there; `onFocus(null)` starts that wait again. Otherwise she faces the camera.
- `onTurn(dxPx, ended)`: `dxPx` is the finger's horizontal move since the last call (as the ui sends it). 1 degree per 2 px, at most 170 degrees from facing the camera, through `princess.turnBy(delta)` with a positive angle for a finger moving right; on the end she faces the camera again (`face()` eases back).
- `onPreviewTap()`: `wave`.
- `portraitFrame(her, row, screen)` is pure and unit tested. `screen.panelLeft` comes from `panelLeftOf(w, h)`, the panel's left edge from the ui's `wardrobeLayout`. A window resize frames again at once.

## The afternoon (`quests.ts`, `quest-kit.ts`, `corner.ts`; docs/v4/2.3.md section 17)

- Free play starts with the bubbles (`layer.offer`): the picnic over the rug's east end (1.2 m east of its centre, y 1.3, v3's `words/picnic.webp`) and this play's maths quest, the hay rack (y 2.3, `items/item-hay-bundle.webp`) or the bead tree (y 3.0, v3's `words/bead.webp` tinted pink). Each carries a `badge`: the swatch of the first gift its quest can still give (`giftSetFor`, `stillToEarn`), none once every set is full. While a place is off screen its bubble waits at the screen edge (the layer does this). In the afternoon the bubbles and the gift cards keep clear of Princess Emma's screen box (`layer.keepClear`), and bubbles keep clear of each other: an edge bubble moves along its edge, one on screen moves up. "When you see a bubble, someone is waiting for you." plays once per play, as in 2.2 (`audio.sayWhole`, said again after a cut by the turn line).
- **The maths quest's rotation** (`mathsQuestFor`): `save.afternoon` keeps the last play's maths quest and the quests she finished. This play's is the last one again when she did not finish it, else the other one; a save without it (2.2's, a first play) gets the bead tree. `?quest=rack|beads` sets it. The afternoon is saved when free play ends.
- A quest starts on a bubble tap, a tap on its place (`WorldPick` `picnic`, `rack`, `beads`, `corner`; the gazebo too while the trunk bubble shows) or when she walks to within 2.5 m of its place (`PLACES`; the nearest place counts, and only when she comes to it, so after the Corner she does not start the picnic 1.9 m away). `?quest=` asks it at once.
- **Every quest resolves framed** (`RUN`, `runQuest`): it walks, frames and runs its moments, and resolves with a release function while the camera, the guests, the followers and the herd are still aside. The afternoon then runs the gift picker in that frame (`giveGift`), then the release. The Corner runs its own picker after its gate.
- **Gifts** (`giveGift`, `wearGift`): `giftOffer(quest, owned, randFor('gift-' + quest))` gives one or two items from the quest's own set, or once it is full from the set with the most items left. `layer.gift` lets her pick ("Pick one present!"). When `layer.gift` resolves (its sparkles have landed, about 1.6 s after the pick) the item is saved in `owned` and on her look, and she keeps wearing it (`wearing`); she twirls, the synthesised fanfare plays (`synth`, never while anyone speaks) and sparkles fly from her to the trunk. When the pick filled the set the gift came from (items left before it, none after: `fillsSet`), Lumi says `lumi4-sticker`, so once per set. No picker when every set is full.
- **The trunk bubble** shows after her first finished quest, once its gift is picked: v3's `items/place-dressing.webp` just over the trunk (y 1.1), with a hair gift's badge like every bubble. The first time a play Lumi says `lumi-dressing-open`. It stays until the Corner is done or free play ends.
- **The clocks** (`tickFree`, docs 17.3): `freeMs` runs while no quest runs and while the Corner's music box plays (`q.dressing`, from `onBox(true)` to `onBox(false)`), and that music box time is also `clock.dressMs.corner`. No picnic or maths quest starts once `freeMs - dressMs.corner` reaches 90 s (`QUEST_CUTOFF_MS`; their bubbles go); the Corner may start while free play is on. Free time stands still while Lumi or Elizabeth speaks outside a quest, at most 45 s a play (`TALK_MAX_MS`). Free play ends when `freeMs` reaches 120 s, no quest runs, and no picnic or maths bubble still shows or the cutoff has passed (`freePlayOver`, `FREE_PLAY_MS`; the Corner runs until its wardrobe closes, so bedtime never starts while she dresses). The Corner's music box gets `clamp(120 s - freeMs, 30 s, 60 s)` (`cornerLimitMs`), so free time is at most 150 s. `wallMs` is for the log only. `skip()` ends only free play's wait, never a quest.
- Quiet prompts every 30 s without a touch, in turn: slide, follow, basket, bubbles (while a bubble shows) and Lumi's Corner line (while the trunk bubble shows).
- The picnic and the hay rack as in 2.2 (below); the hay rack only when it is this play's maths quest. The bead tree's moment is the patterns lane's (`game/beads.ts`).

### The Dressing Corner (`corner.ts`, 17.4)

1. Her babies stop following and walk to `CORNER.babies` (put there after 4 s, the last step towards the camera); her other followers wait at `CORNER.aside`; the herd near the step walks behind the camera (`herdAsideAt(g, CORNER.herd, true)`). She walks to `CORNER.stand`.
2. `frameAt` the step: centre (-14, 3.0), 5.5 m wide, the first baby's head top at 45%, her crown kept.
3. Lumi: `lumi4-corner` "Your babies want to dress up first!"
4. The gate: `gateWish` asks `planWish(g, 'phrase', 'gate1' | 'gate2', { wish: false })`, then `runWish(..., { frame: false })`. With fewer than 6 strong phrases among the pairs `?phrases=` allows (every ok pair minus the iPad's crosses by default, so only after many crosses or with `?phrases=emma` before her check) the reading lane's planner gives no phrase: it gives the dress-up wish itself or throws because it has no pair to pick. Then the gate asks for `planWish(g, 'dress', part, { wish: false })` itself, for both reads, and "Your baby wants to dress up like you!" plays before the first. So the Corner always opens. `q.gate` keeps the kind (word when a read was a word wish) and the first tries for the `kind=play` line.
5. The gift from the hair set (`giveGift(g, 'corner')`): she wears it into the wardrobe.
6. Her babies step to `CORNER.watch` (outside her close-up and at least 1.2 m from the followers' spots), Lumi's portrait steps away, the trunk lid opens (`world.corner.lid(true)`), and `dressUp` (wardrobe-part.ts) opens the wardrobe at her spot in the portrait frame with `board` (`boardSpec`: what she owns, today's gifts, the ribbons, each set's quest picture) and `limitMs` (`?box=` or `cornerLimitMs`). `onBox` sets `q.dressing`. The Corner's panel says no `g4-ready` and no idle line ("When you are ready, tap play."): those are the start mirror's (`dressClips`), and a pause at the Corner is her dressing time.
7. The kind ending is `dressUp`'s: her look saved as it is, a `kind=look` line with `at=corner`, the curtsy while the layer chimes and says "Lovely!". The lid closes, and the quest resolves with its release (the frame, the herd home, the babies follow, Lumi back).
- **The trunk after her visit** (`trunkTap`): a tap on the trunk, the mirror or the step opens the lid on `layer.board` for 3.5 s, then closes it. It never refuses and never starts the Corner again.
- **The saddle sign** (input.ts): a tap makes the picture shimmer with a sparkle, and Lumi says `lumi4-soon` once a play (never over a line that plays).

### The picnic and the hay rack (2.2.0)

- The basket line and the food basket as before (a bubble tapped during the explanation skips the line; the basket shows after the quest). A food dropped on a unicorn plays the synthesised munch (2.3.0). If the hay rack is this play's maths quest, the three grazing herd unicorns nearest the rack walk to its eat spots; the one picked by `randFor('rack-herd')` hops and says "I'm hungry!" as an aside when it arrives.
- The picnic: her babies stop following, her other followers wait west of the rug (`PICNIC.aside`, clear of the cushions and the close-up), the herd grazing by the gazebo walks behind the camera (`herdAside(g, true, { walk: true })`). She walks to `PICNIC.stand` (at once after 12 s), the babies to the seats (placed there after 4 s, facing the camera). The frame centres the rug 0.6 m east, 5.5 m wide, head tops at 45%. Nothing is said first (no `lumi4-picnic`, which pointed at the foods), then a wish per guest from the stream `picnic` with intro and goal `g4-give` (no warm-up; results `picnic1`, `picnic2`, strand `r`). The reward: `lumi4-picnic-done`, stars on her head, `princess.extra('rose')`, a twirl and hearts. The rose stays until the end of the play (`snapTo` takes it off). Then the gift (a crown).
- The hay rack: she walks to her spot 2.2 m west of the rack (at `HAY_RACK.stand` she stood between the camera and the rack, behind the hay panel) and turns half to the camera; the sum from the stream `rack` in the current band. Frame: 0.3 m west of the rack centre (clear of the stone tray), 6 m wide, the rack's top (y 1.5) at 28% of the height. `react('munch')` (too many; `'hot'` too) makes a helper munch, `'look'` (too few; `'cold'` too) makes the helpers look, `'right'` (or `'warm'`) fills the rack (`stable.hay(1, 1200)`) and every helper and both babies munch with hearts. 3 s later the gift (shoes), then the helpers graze at home again. Result `rack`, strand `m`; it moves the maths band.

## Sounds the level plays (2.3.0, through `audio.fx`)

- `crown-twinkle` as the crown appears at the welcome, before Lumi's crown line (first play) or "An egg is waiting." (later plays); `bedtime-bell` after the dusk fade, before "The sun is going down."; `owl` after the goodnight sky shot, before Lumi's ribbons line; `stable-door` when a stable door swings open (world/stable.ts, at most once per 1.5 s). Each line awaits its effect (at most 1 s); a missing file is silent.

## Reading level, maths band, streams and the save

- `reading.ts`: a first play starts at level 2; three `first` in a row move up, two `miss` or `helped` in a row move down; the history clears after each step. The play's first wish is a level lower. `?rl=` forces a level for every reading moment and the fold is then not stored.
- Every reading result (three wishes, two picnic guests) folds into `save.reading` and is written at once.
- `band.ts`: the first maths moment of a play is band A; `first` moves to B, `helped` to A, a miss and the doors leave it. `?maths=` fixes it. The total of a sum is `need` for a warm egg and the hay rack and `have` for a snow egg; every planned sum goes into `g.maths.used`.
- `streams.ts`: `g.randFor(label)` is `stream(seed, label)`, cached for the play (asking again continues it); a jump resets every stream. Labels: `egg1`, `egg2`, `rack`, `rack-herd`, `hatch1`, `hatch2`, `naming1`, `naming2`, `wish1`, `pond`, `wish2`, `picnic`, `rollcall`, `visit`. A jump's skipped babies use `hatch1`/`hatch2` and `naming1`/`naming2`, so they match a full play.
- `save.ts`: `createSave(() => localStorage, fresh)`; `parseSave` is pure and tolerant (junk, an empty store, a newer `v`, unknown ids, storage that throws); every access is in try/catch. Writes: on Play (`plays + 1`), 300 ms after a released pick, on Done, after each reading result, at each gift (what she owns and her look), when free play ends (`afternoon`), at the goodnight gift, at the end screen (`last`). Pink is always owned and never listed in `ribbons`.

## Results and the server log

- `Results.add` logs `[mmw result]` and sends `diag.txt?kind=result&…` (at most 400 characters, `diagQuery`). Wishes: `part`, `o`, `ms`, `mi`, `lv` (level), `n` (basket size), `w`, `pt` (print touched), `q` (quick), `tried` as `word:decoy`. Maths: `band`, `k`, `need`, `have`, `a`, `st` (stones), `pl` (the answer's place, from 0), `q`, `tried` as `stone:decoy`, `dc` (every decoy). `pt` and `q` are read from any `hooks.log` event whose data has `printTouched` or `quick` booleans.
- Done in the customiser (or the end of its music box) sends `kind=look` with `at` (mirror or corner), `end` (done or time), `tabs` (the tabs she picked in), `ms` and the look's short fields.
- **`kind=play`** (2.3.0, `results.ts playFields`, sent at goodnight before the end screen): `v` the build's version, `pl` plays, `min` minutes since Play, `ck` checked s, `dr` dressing s (`mi` + `co`), `mi` mirror s, `co` the Corner's dressing s (its music box, `clock.dressMs.corner`), `cv` the whole Corner visit s (the walk, the gate, the gift and the wardrobe; its gate is checked time too), `fr` free s, `gk` the gate's kind (phrase, or word before Emma's picture check), `gt` gate first tries, `q` quests finished, `rl` reading level, `ph` phrase level, `mb` maths band, `pt` pattern level, `gf` gifts as field:id (last, so a long list is cut first). Under 400 characters (tested).
- Those requests 404 on every server (that is the point: the request line is the record). `flows/lib.sh` `finish()` ignores their 404 console lines; any other `diag.txt` line (a failed picture or clip) still fails a flow.

## Test hook additions (`window.__game`)

- `state()` adds `look`, `wardrobe` (the layer's `snapshot().wardrobe`, or null), `readingLevel`, `mathsBand` and `quests` (`offered`, `done`, `running`, `helpers`, `rose`, `hay`, `freeMs`; 2.3.0: `maths`, `dressing`, `gifts`, `over`, `cut`, `gate`, `boards`, `signSaid`, `signs`, `cornerMs`, `lid`), `owned`, `clock`, `sound` and `framedAt` (the frame the camera holds, or null). Part `wardrobe` comes first.
- `spec()`: the open moment as the game planned it. Eggs and the rack: the spec without clips (`stones`, `decoys`, `band`, `skin`). Wishes: `kind`, `level`, `target`, `basket`, `decoys`. Naming: `bells`, and once chosen `{ name, gems, distractors }` with the gems in slot order.
- `setLook(partial)` (applies and saves), `setRibbons(list)` (the earned colours; the customiser reads them when it opens, so `jump('wardrobe')` after it), `save()` (reads the store again), `clearSave()`.
- 3D targets `rack` and `picnic`; 2.3.0: `trunk`, `mirror`, `bead tree` and `sign`. `placeAt(x, z)` puts her at a ground point with the camera (the flows measure the budget there).

## Rules in the code

- A tap never cuts speech: a unicorn's name or "Yum!" waits its turn (`sayAside`), one at a time; further taps only show the tag and the hop.
- `setTime(g, t, ms)` sets the time of day in the world and the colour wash in the 2D layer (`layer.sky`) together.
- Results: `detail.tried` collects every wrong choice (`hooks.log('tried', { what })` from the ui moments, the door number from the door moments). A right door after the row hint (level 2) is `helped`.
- A wrong door: the unicorn inside in a nightcap ("Not me!"); a unicorn of that door still in the yard trots to its door and shakes its head ("Not me!"); an empty stall swings open and her baby giggles ("Silly!"). During a door moment a tap on a waiting unicorn makes it hop and look at its own door.
- `world.onSlow(level => actors.setDetail(level))`; the `?perf=1` box shows the pixel ratio and the quality level.
- No WebGL 2, or `createWorld` throws: `window.__mmwFallback()` from `index.html` shows a night picture with the home button. The same script shows it after 10 s when the game script never started (`main.ts` sets `window.__mmwStarted` first thing).
- Baby close-ups (naming and wishes) pass her crown to `frameAt` as `keep`, so her crown stays on screen.
- A picture shown by a baby (`showToy` in `moments.ts`) is taken away after its time only if no other picture replaced it meanwhile. A greyscale picture shows in its tint (`WishItem.tint`).
- World touches (`input.ts`) run on `press()`: a release only the window hears still taps; a cancel, a lost capture or the next touch ends the touch without a tap, and a carried unicorn lands where the finger last was. One lost release never blocks later world taps.
- During a side quest a Lumi tap replays an open moment's goal; between the quest's moments she stays quiet (the basket is hidden).
