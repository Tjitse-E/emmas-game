Phrase pictures: the tinted picture check (2.3)

The Dressing Corner's phrase wish ("the green sock") shows these 30 pictures: every phrase colour on every phrase thing, in the colour's picture tint. Show them to Emma and ask what each one is ("What colour is it? What is it?"). Gold against yellow above all: the dashed yellow row is not in the game; it is there so she can say whether gold looks different from yellow. On her own iPad the check needs no rebuild: open check.html next to the game and tap a tick or a cross for each picture; a tick counts as Emma's check at once, a cross keeps the pair out. To keep a verdict in the code, a pair she names right becomes "by": "emma" in tools/phrase-check.json (or "verdict": "drop" when she does not know it); then run node tools/build-content.ts. Until at least 6 phrases are strong among Emma's pairs, the gate plays two dress-up word wishes. Tap a picture to hear its phrase (its word until the phrase clip is made). With the words hidden for Emma, taps are silent.
