Sound check

Every effect, the day loop and the music box of Moon Meadow World 2.3.0, with the rules that keep them away from Elizabeth. Tap Start first (the iPad needs a tap before it plays sound). This page plays files that are not approved yet.

Effects

Play plays the file, or the fallback the game uses while the file is missing. Approve a file after listening: sh games/moon-meadow-world/tools/audio/sfx.sh --approve id.

Rules

Each button plays a short scene. What you should hear is under it.

Day loop

0.22 by day, 0.08 under speech, silent in a checked moment (out in 400 ms, back 1.5 s after). Night is quiet in 2.3.0.

Music box

An original tune made in code. It slows down in its last four bars and ends on its home note when the time is up. It goes down to 0.1 under speech.
