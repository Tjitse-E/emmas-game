# Moon Meadow World (v4 demo)

One playable level of v4 in 3D: "Emma's first day in Moon Meadow" ([docs/v4/demo.md](../../docs/v4/demo.md), with the 2.2.0 changes in [docs/v4/2.2.md](../../docs/v4/2.2.md), the 2.3.0 changes in [docs/v4/2.3.md](../../docs/v4/2.3.md) and the 2.4.0 changes in [docs/v4/2.4.md](../../docs/v4/2.4.md)). A play has five parts: the wardrobe, the welcome, the hub, bedtime and goodnight. After Play, Emma dresses Princess Emma at the start mirror for at most 45 s while a music box plays: six picture tabs (hair, eyes and jewels, gown, crown, shoes, hair things), each with its rails of shapes and colours, next to a close-up of her in 3D, and the big pink play button when she is ready. Elizabeth says each colour she picks. After Lumi's welcome she chooses where to go (the hub, docs/v4/2.4.md section 5.2): three or four glowing bubbles, each over a place, and she taps one or walks to its place. Up to three are places where she reads or counts, picked so that reading and maths both show while both are left, and at most one is a play place. After each place the empty bubbles fill again. The places are the nest egg and the shore egg (adding under a blanket at the nest, taking away at the shore: three sums each, all within 10 as at her school, an easy one to 7 or 8, a middle one to 9 and a bond to 10, with 4 or 5 stones of 2 to 9 that wake when Elizabeth has said both numbers and a heart on the panel per right sum; then the hatch, naming the baby with letter gems and its first wish), a herd baby's wish, the royal picnic, this play's maths quest, the ride, and the two play places: the Dressing Corner (once a play) and the pond with its slide. Each finished learning place earns one play bubble. A pond visit lasts 3 minutes or 6 slides; between visits the slide rests while three ducks swim under the chute. On the ride she taps a saddle in the paddock in the north-east and rides Bella or Hazel side-saddle through a gate along a trail, with up to two babies behind: at the fork she reads two signposts to choose the road, and at the second clearing a star egg asks three sums of more and fewer within 10 and hatches a wild baby. She rides at most twice a play, so up to four babies hatch: two from the eggs at home and two from the trail. On her first play ever the welcome ends with the nest egg, and the bubbles come after its wish. Bedtime starts at 30 minutes of play time (the start mirror and the Corner's music box do not count, and a place that runs finishes first), or earlier only once every learning place is done or past its time ("done early": a last pond visit and the Corner if she has not been, then bedtime). She puts the herd and her babies to bed by reading the door numbers: with babies two doors from 7 to 12 at the roll call and a visit to a door from 11 to 12 + n, with none three doors and no visit. The end screen shows the whole herd in their own colours, with her babies and their names on top. A play takes about 36 minutes (docs/v4/2.4.md section 5.7) and ends with a new ribbon colour and goodnight.

The gift quests of 2.3.0 ([docs/v4/2.3.md](../../docs/v4/2.3.md) section 17) are hub places: the royal picnic (two wish words and a crown present) and one maths quest a play, the bead tree (three gaps in a bead pattern, earrings or a necklace) or the hay rack (one sum within 10, never a sum the play asked; shoes), in turn (the one she did not finish comes again). Every finished quest gives a present: she picks one of two new things, wears it and keeps it. At the Dressing Corner at the gazebo's step two babies dress up first (two phrase reads such as "the green sock"; two dress-up words only when fewer than 6 phrases are strong), she gets a hair present, then she dresses at the trunk and the mirror while a music box plays for 60 s, off the play clock. After her visit a tap on the trunk shows her treasure board. The saddle sign stands by the paddock's west side. Each place sends a `kind=hub` line as it ends, and each play one `kind=play` line at goodnight (see "Watch Emma play").

Her look, the things she owns, her ribbon colours, hidden reading, phrase, pattern and sign levels and the last play's words and sums are saved in the browser (`localStorage`, key `mmw.save2` since 2.3.0). Since 2.4.0 the words, the sums and the maths quest are saved after each place, so a play she leaves halfway still counts; the hub, the herd and the babies are not saved, and the next play starts fresh. 2.2's `mmw.save` is read only while `mmw.save2` is missing or broken, and is never written or removed. On the next play she taps Play, sees her saved look in the customiser and taps the play button once; Lumi's welcome is shorter and the bubbles come at once. Each address (Emma's server, a test server, another host name) has its own save, and Safari may clear it after 7 days without a visit: she then starts fresh with the default look and the middle reading level.

The demo is throwaway. It shows Tjitse how v4 plays, whether Emma likes it, and how her iPad (8th generation, STACK.md) copes with up to 18 code-built unicorns near her (the herd, four babies and the two mounts), the pond, the slide and the trail. Vite 8, TypeScript 6 and three.js 0.186.1, built for Safari 15. Speech is pre-generated ElevenLabs audio: v3's clips and pictures from `games/moon-meadow-home/`, and the demo's own 207 clips in `public/audio/v4demo/`. Sound effects and the day loop are in `public/audio/sfx/`.

## Address options

- `?perf=1` shows frames per second, draw calls, triangles, geometries, textures, unicorn detail, the pixel ratio (`pr`) and the quality level (`q0` full, `q1` lower pixel ratio, `q2` cheaper herd too) in a small box at the top. Use it on her iPad, with Low Power Mode off (it caps Safari at 30 frames per second).
- `?pr=<n>` caps the pixel ratio (default 1.5; `?pr=1` draws a quarter fewer pixels).
- `?aa=0` turns antialiasing off.
- `?at=<part>` starts at a part after the Play button, without the opening sweep: `wardrobe` (the customiser), `welcome`, `hub`, `bedtime`, `goodnight`. Any part after `wardrobe` skips the customiser and uses her saved look. 2.4.0: the old part names are aliases of a hub start (docs/v4/2.4.md section 8). `egg1` is `?at=hub&place=egg`, `naming1` and `wish1` add `&step=naming` and `&step=wish`; `egg2`, `naming2` and `wish2` are the same with `place=shore` and the nest egg counted as done; `pond` is `place=pond`; `free` is the hub with both home eggs done, as free play was. The babies of the places a start counts as done are there, named with the first name of their bells, and follow her. Any other value plays from the start.
- `?place=<place>` (2.4.0, with `?at=hub` or an alias) asks that place as the hub opens, earned or not: `egg`, `shore`, `wish`, `picnic`, `beads`, `rack`, `ride`, `corner` or `pond`. `shore` lands the shore egg at once; `corner` and `pond` count as earned. `?quest=` is its alias, so `?at=free&quest=rack` still works. `beads` or `rack` also makes it this play's maths quest; without `?at=` that is all it does.
- `?step=<step>` (2.4.0, with `?at=hub&place=`) jumps into a step of the place. An egg place (`place=egg` or `shore`): `naming` or `wish` (its baby's first wish). The ride (`place=ride`): trip 1, already on the mount, at the fork for `sign2` and for `egg` (it rides on to clearing 2 and the star egg), at clearing 2 for `naming` and `wish`, at the gate for `home`. In a trip `step=wish` starts at the naming (notes/ride.md). Skipped signs change no sign level.
- `?babies=0` to `4` (2.4.0, with `?at=`) starts with that many named babies (the nest's, the shore's and two from the trail, doors 13 to 16; up to three follow her), in place of the babies of the places counted as done. Bedtime then has 12 + n unicorns.
- `?bed=<seconds>` (2.4.0) puts bedtime at that much play time instead of 30 minutes (the flows). Every cutoff moves with it: 4, 3 and 1 minutes before it no ride, no egg and no place starts, done early's last check is 2 minutes before it and its wait 2 minutes. Under 5 minutes they are 87%, 90%, 97% and 93% of it and the wait 7%.
- `?sign=1`, `?sign=2` or `?sign=3` plays every signpost at that level and does not store the level (2.4.0).
- `?mount=bella` or `?mount=hazel` is the mount a jump into a trip rides (Bella without it) (2.4.0).
- `?seed=<n>` plays the same level every time (same eggs, names, words, signs and doors) when she makes the same choices. Each moment has its own random stream, named by place and count since 2.4.0 (`egg-shore`, `naming-2`, `sign-1`), so the dressing and the walks never shift a sum or a word. A jump plans none of the places it skips, and since 2.4.0 a play leaves out the sums it has asked, so `?at=egg2&seed=5` asks the shore egg as if no sum came before it.
- `?fresh=1` keeps the save in memory only: every load is a first visit with the default look, and nothing Emma has saved is read or changed. The flows use it.
- `?rl=1`, `?rl=2` or `?rl=3` plays every wish at that reading level (4 items at level 1, 5 at levels 2 and 3), without the warm-up, and does not store the level.
- `?maths=a` or `?maths=b` fixes the maths band (4 stones, or 5 stones; the hay rack's sums also reach 10 in band B).
- `?contrast=0` brings back v3's rule for a wrong wish item: the sounds of the word at once, instead of the wrong item's word with the letters that differ.
- `?pl=1`, `?pl=2` or `?pl=3` plays every bead tree pattern at that level and does not store it (2.3.0).
- `?box=<seconds>` makes the wardrobe's music box this long at the start mirror and the Dressing Corner (the flows use 5) (2.3.0).
- `?phrases=emma` or `?phrases=all` sets which tinted pictures the Dressing Corner's phrase wish uses. Without it every picture with an ok check plays, Claude's checks too, minus each one crossed on `check.html` on that iPad: 20 strong phrases on a fresh device. `?phrases=emma` is 2.3.0's rule, only the pictures Emma has checked. `?phrases=all` takes every ok picture and ignores the iPad's crosses (the flows and dev). With fewer than 6 strong phrases the gate plays two dress-up word wishes (2.3.0).
- `check.html` (next to `index.html`, also in `dist/`) is Emma's picture check of the 30 phrase pictures on her own iPad: Tjitse taps a tick or a cross after she names each one. A cross keeps the pair out of the game at once, without a rebuild; a tick records her check (it counts for `?phrases=emma`). The verdicts stay on that iPad and that address (`mmw.phrasecheck1`, never her save). The page says how many phrases are on now: 20 with no cross, the gate plays phrase wishes from 6 and below that two dress-up word wishes. With every picture of one target colour crossed (green, gold, blue or brown), or the red and pink pictures crossed, none are on; three crosses on one colour can already leave fewer than 6, and the page then says why. 2.4.0 adds the 13 sign words under the phrases, with no buttons: Tjitse tells Claude which ones she does not name, and such a word leaves the signposts in a patch.
- `?portrait=0` keeps her normal detail in the customiser's close-up (compare the frame rate with `?perf=1`).

Without `?pr=`, a slow iPad lowers the quality by itself: first the pixel ratio, then the herd's detail. The console says `[mmw] quality lowered`.

If WebGL 2 does not start, a night picture with the home button shows instead, and the console says `[mmw] no WebGL 2`. The same picture shows when the game script has not started after 10 s (an old Safari, a file that did not load). A slow start on her iPad does not trigger it.

Every checked moment (each egg's 3 sums, each naming, each wish, the picnic's 2 guests, the hay rack's sum, the 3 bead gaps, the Dressing Corner's 2 gate reads, the 2 signposts of a trip and the door numbers) is logged to the console as `[mmw result]` with first try, miss or helped, and `detail.tried` lists what she chose wrongly (stones, words, names, cards, doors). A right answer after the focused hint is helped; a right item after only the light contrast is a miss. Each result also goes to the server's request log as `diag.txt?kind=result&…` (the file does not exist; Emma's server logs the request), with the play number (`pn`, 2.4.0), the reading level, the basket size, the word, what she tried and why it was in the basket, the maths band, the stones and the answer's place, and whether she picked within 1.5 s (`q=1`). Done in the customiser sends one `kind=look` line with the rows she touched, the time and her look. `window.__game` is the test hook (state, targets, answer, the open moment's spec, jump, perf, missing clips, setLook, setOwned, placeAt, save, clearSave; 2.4.0 adds setPlayMs, setHub, setSave, playLine, gather, screenOf and occluded).

## Run it on the Mac

```sh
cd games/moon-meadow-world
npm run dev                  # http://localhost:5173/, v3's clips come from /v3/
npm test                     # unit tests of the four parts
npm run check                # TypeScript
bash flows/run.sh            # build, test server on port 8785, all flows, stop (outside the Claude Code sandbox)
MMW_LONG=1 bash flows/run.sh # only the long play, about 36 minutes
```

`flows/run.sh` runs `level`, `dress`, `quest`, `smoke`, `ride` and `bedtime`, in that order. The level flow plays the whole level at 1080 x 810 (Emma's iPad) with a fresh save and real taps and drags: Done in the customiser at once, the long welcome, the hub's forced nest egg with one wrong answer, naming 1 and the dress-up wish with one wrong each, the four bubbles with the first hub's explanation, the picnic from its bubble, the shore egg landing with its sums, naming and wish, a minute with no leak, every bubble gone at 29:00 with "Soon it is bedtime!" and bedtime at 30:00 (the flow sets the play clock), the roll call and the visit with one wrong door each, and goodnight; then play 2 on a real save, with the short welcome and the opening set (the nest egg, the ride and the picnic). It checks the results log per place with what she tried and the play number, the basket and stone counts, that no letter sound of the name star plays under "Listen again" or the name, the save after goodnight, the frame budget, that no clip is missing and that the console has no errors. `dress.flow.sh` checks the wardrobe at every size: every tab, rail and swatch touchable, a tab acting on pointerdown, one pick per field, a slide released off the panel, a pointercancel mid-slide, the budget in the body and face frames, Done before the music box ends, a finger held on a rail when the box ends (`?box=5`), and the saved look and the crown tab after a reload. `quest.flow.sh` plays the gift quests as hub places (`?at=hub&place=`) at 1080 x 810 and 1080 x 740: the bubbles, the budget in the follow view at the Dressing Corner, the bead tree and the saddle sign, the Corner (two gate reads with one wrong, the hair present saved and worn, the trunk lid and its board, the budget in its wardrobe at 1080 x 810, 1080 x 740 and 1024 x 690, a trunk tap after the visit), the gate's two dress-up word wishes with `?phrases=emma`, the bead tree and its jewel present, the picnic and the hay rack with their presents, and the maths quest's rotation over three loads on a real save. In a lane worktree `run.sh` serves the main checkout's v3 test build, read only, as worktrees have no build of v3. The smoke flow starts at the customiser, at the old part names (the aliases), at the hub with a bubble tap, at four bubbles on one edge, at the pond with a baby and the resting slide with its ducks, at the meadow with every unicorn near her, at bedtime and at goodnight, at 1080 x 810, 1080 x 740 (Safari's bars), 1024 x 768, 1024 x 690 (the Air 2), 1180 x 820 and 1366 x 1024, and checks that every target, and every 3D unicorn near her, is on screen and at least 64 px. At the pond it first leaves two world touches without a release on the canvas, as her iPad can, and the walk that follows must still work.

`ride.flow.sh` (2.4.0) rides from `?at=hub&place=ride` with three followers: the saddle icons on screen and at least 64 px, nothing hiding her, the saddles or the icons, a palm on the screen, getting on Bella side-saddle, the roads and slots shown only from getting on to getting off, the budget at the paddock with both mounts and three followers and on the gallop, out and home through the gate, and getting off at her stand. With `MMW_RIDE_PART=all` (the default) it also plays the fork with one wrong card, sign 2, both clearings, the star egg's three sums and the naming on the trail, checks that no slot shows a word while it is on screen at a signpost, and reloads during sign 2 (no sign level changes, the next play starts at the hub). `MMW_RIDE_PART=paddock` rides out and back without the trail's checks. `bedtime.flow.sh` (2.4.0) plays bedtime with `?babies=0`, `2` and `4`: with no baby "The stable is this way.", three roll call doors and no visit, with babies two doors and a visit, the babies moving in at 13 to 12 + n, the stalls, the count at goodnight, the stable at night with 16 heads within the budget, the trail hidden at night and `bb` on the play line. With `MMW_BEDTIME_PART=all` (the default) it also checks the clock with `?bed=90`: "Soon it is bedtime!" at 87.3 s of play time, bedtime at 90 s, and a picnic that runs at bedtime finishing first. `MMW_BEDTIME_PART=doors` runs only the doors.

`MMW_LONG=1`, `MMW_LONG=pond` or `MMW_LONG=slide` (2.4.0) runs only `long.flow.sh` at 1080 x 810: a play 2 on a fresh save from the short welcome to goodnight at bedtime, about 36 minutes, at a child's pace. `1` takes the nearest bubble first (play B of docs/v4/2.4.md section 5.7), `pond` is the pond-lover (every play bubble at once, staying until each visit ends), `slide` the child who only taps the slide. After every place it checks that the page never reloaded, the console has no errors, the run's deferred list is level, and the geometry and texture counts never grow after a kind of place she has done before. At the end it reads the `kind=play` line and checks the history's targets (`1`: 600 checked seconds or more, reading and spelling 10 or more, maths 12 or more, no more pond visits than learning places, bedtime between 29.5 and 31.8 minutes; `pond`: at most 5 visits and 15 minutes at the pond, the same reading and maths; `slide`: no visit, no reading, only the 3 roll call doors). `LONG_BED=<seconds>` plays to an earlier bedtime and skips the count checks (a quick run of the driver).

`MMW_FLOWS="level smoke"` runs only those flows, in that order (2.4.0); the `MMW_NO_*` switches still leave a flow out. The level flow runs at 1080 x 810, dress and smoke at every size, quest, ride and bedtime at 1080 x 810 and 1080 x 740. `MMW_FULL=all` plays the level, ride and bedtime flows at every size, `MMW_SIZES="1080x740"` runs only the sizes given, `MMW_NO_LEVEL=1`, `MMW_NO_DRESS=1`, `MMW_NO_QUEST=1` and `MMW_NO_SMOKE=1` leave a flow out, and `MMW_SKIP_BUILD=1` reuses `dist/`. `MMW_PORT` and `MMW_SESSION` give a run its own port and browser (never 8765), and `MMW_VITE_CACHE` its own vite cache, so runs can go side by side; `run.sh` stops when the port already answers, as another run's server would. A missing clip whose line waits for `gen.sh` passes and is listed, unless `MMW_STRICT_CLIPS=1`. Screenshots and the budget lines (`budget.txt`) go to `$TMPDIR/mmw-shots`, or `$TMPDIR/mmw-shots-<session>` with `MMW_SESSION`.

The four parts and their notes: the world (`src/world/`, [notes/world.md](notes/world.md)), the characters (`src/actors/`, [notes/actors.md](notes/actors.md)), the 2D layer (`src/ui/`, [notes/ui.md](notes/ui.md)) and the content (`src/content/`, [notes/content.md](notes/content.md)). Sit next to her and note. New in 2.4.0:

- Does she understand the bubbles without help? What does she pick first, and does she come back to reading and maths after the pond? The `kind=hub` lines and `sq` on the play line show her order.
- Does she wait in the hub? After 90 s without starting a place the nearest learning bubble grows and bobs and its "this way" line plays. Does she follow it?
- Does she ride? Does she find the paddock in the north-east and tap a saddle? Is the trail too long for her?
- Does she read the signs or guess? Compare her first tries per level in the `sign` result lines (`k=sign`: `lv` the level, `w` the word Elizabeth asked for, `ws` the three cards, `tried` the cards she tapped first).

- Does the resting slide with the ducks upset her, or does she go on to a bubble?
- Does she reach bedtime, and is 30 minutes of play too long for her?

How to read the lines (docs/v4/2.4.md section 5.8):

- `kind=hub` goes out as each place ends: `pn` the play number, `p` the place's letter (e the nest egg, s the shore egg, w a herd baby's wish, p the picnic, b the bead tree, k the hay rack, r the ride, o the pond, c the Corner), `n` its count this play, `min` the play clock in minutes, `t` the place's seconds and `ck` its checked seconds. A play she leaves before goodnight still shows what she chose.
- `kind=play` keeps 2.3's fields and adds `nr`, `ns` and `nm` (reading, spelling and maths moments), `r1`, `s1` and `m1` (their first tries), `sq` (the places in the order she chose them, the same letters), `pv` (pond visits), `po` (seconds at the pond), `ri` (seconds on a mount), `bt` (the play clock in minutes when bedtime began), `bb` (babies hatched) and `sg` (the sign level at the end). `fr` now means seconds in the hub with no place running. Its `min` counts from the Play tap, dressing included.
- The aims over three plays: `ck` / 60 is 10 checked minutes or more; `ck` over `min` × 60 is 30% or more; `dr` over `min` × 60 is 10% or less; `nr` + `ns` is 10 or more and `nm` 12 or more; `pv` is no more than the learning letters in `sq` (all but o and c); `sq` has learning letters between the play letters; `bt` is near 30, or under it only after done early. A play under these aims shows a child who skips places.

The `kind=look` line shows the rows she touched and how long she took. The ribbon tap (on the hair thing she wears) only does something once she owns a second colour.
- The baskets of 4 and 5: does she read before she drags, or pick at once (`q=1`)? After a wrong item, does she look at its word and the gold letters (the contrast), or wait for the sounds (`pt=1` when she touches a sound dot first)?
- The sleeping stones: do they read as "wait"? They wake with a hop when Elizabeth has said both numbers. A first stone touch within 1.5 s of the wake shows as `q=1`.
- The quests: does she find their bubbles, and take the picnic and the hay rack? Skipping them is fine.
- On the second play: is the short welcome enough, and does her saved look come back?

From the demo:

- Does she find her way? Does she tap the grass to walk, follow the sparkle beacon and the arrow at the screen edge, and get to the nest, the pond, the wild egg and the stable without your help?
- Is the maths hard but doable? Note the egg sums and the door numbers she gets first time, and where she needs the hint or the ghost hand. The console's `detail.tried` shows near misses (a stone one off, a neighbouring door).
- Are the words easy enough? Does she read the wish word herself, or wait for it to be spoken? Does she spell the names with the gems?
- Does she enjoy the slide? Does she drag the unicorns onto it, slide down herself, and keep playing at the pond?
- Does she find the stable at bedtime? A tap anywhere in the stable yard, on a door or on the stable building takes her there.
- Does she use the hints? A wrong stone lifts the blanket, and the focused hint counts the cups that are still cold. After two wrong names the ghost hand finishes the name with her.
- Does she leave by accident? The home button needs a 1 s press while a pink ring fills; a quick tap only wiggles it.
- Is it smooth on her iPad? Open `?perf=1` once and note the frame rate in the meadow, at the pond and at the stable at night. If `q` goes to 1 or 2, the iPad lowered the quality by itself; try `?pr=1&aa=0` and compare.
