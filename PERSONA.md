# Persona: Emma

- **Name:** Emma
- **Age:** 6
- **School:** International school in Cyprus, British system, primary (Year 1 / Year 2).
- **Languages:** Dutch is her native language. She speaks basic English. She is learning to read English.
- **Maths:** Learning basic maths (counting, number recognition, simple adding and taking away).
- **Likes:** horses, unicorns, princesses, kings and queens, Furbies.

## What this means for design

- She cannot rely on reading instructions yet. Every instruction is spoken (ElevenLabs, voice Elizabeth) and shown with pictures.
- She plays on an iPad. Large touch targets, no keyboard, no hover, no tiny text.
- English is her second language. Use simple, common words and a friendly British voice.
- Her favourite themes (horses, unicorns, royalty, Furbies) are the setting of the games. Furbies are a trademark, so we use our own fluffy Furby-like creatures instead of the real characters.
- Short sessions. A round should take a few minutes and always end with a reward.
