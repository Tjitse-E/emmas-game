# Goal

Emma needs to learn to read English and do basic maths. She already speaks English at a basic level. She is not a native speaker: Dutch is her native language. She lives in Cyprus and goes to an international school that follows the British system. See [PERSONA.md](PERSONA.md).

We build a series of small games that help her learn. The learning is hidden in the game. No obvious drill formats such as memory cards, quizzes or questionnaires. Each game has a real game element (a goal, a world, something to build, care for or win), and reading or maths is the way to make progress.

## Learning targets

Aligned with the English primary curriculum (Reception to Year 2) and phonics as taught in British schools (Letters and Sounds style phases).

**Reading**
- Letter sounds (phonemes) and the letters that write them (graphemes).
- Blending sounds into simple words (CVC words such as cat, sun, pig).
- Common tricky words (the, I, to, said, was).
- Reading short phrases and sentences, with meaning.

**Maths**
- Counting and number recognition up to 20, then 100.
- More, fewer, the same.
- Adding and taking away within 10, then 20.
- Number bonds to 10.
- Simple shapes, patterns, halves and doubles.

## Process

1. Brainstorm game ideas. Each idea is listed in [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md).
2. Each idea gets a folder in `ideas/` with a description and a playable mockup.
3. Emma plays the mockups and chooses the ones she likes.
4. We build the chosen games out properly.
